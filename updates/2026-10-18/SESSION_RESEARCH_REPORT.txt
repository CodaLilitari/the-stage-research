Local synthesis by Coda (Codex Lily), collaborating with Richard Pemberton

Paths in this report are relative to the project root, The Stage.

The session advanced the artificial-blood milestone by establishing a concrete
modeling dependency: accessible oxygen delivery depends on release kinetics and
hydraulic constraints, not storage capacity alone. In one selected, refined
hypothetical comparison, an equilibrium carrier slightly improves distal tissue
oxygen over dissolved-only fluid, while the same carrier with finite release
falls below it. Independent numerical work supports that conditional result.
A public measured-data analysis then exposes why an apparent stopped-flow rate
cannot yet be substituted for the model's effective channel-scale rate.

This is progress toward a defensible oxygen-carrier research program, not a
manufactured artificial blood, a formulation, empirical tissue validation or a
clinical result. Human oxygen therapeutics and circulation supporting engineered
tissue on a robotic body remain separate applications. No new public release
was made by this report; the earlier published snapshot remains a separate record.

1. The new transport result: what changed, and under which assumptions

AB-CT0 completed all 78 planned attempts: 54 primary cases, 12 controls and 12
refinements. Its two discretionary reserve attempts were unused. Source and
inputs were frozen and independently reviewed before the authorized execution.
Separate saved-field QA passed 514/514 bounded checks, including conservation,
wall flux, tissue-cell residuals, free/bound inventory, selection, convergence,
hydraulics, manifests and planned/attempted reconciliation. The QA did not rerun
the transport solver. Maximum reconstructed relative segment/global conservation
errors were 3.31e-8 and 4.01e-9, below the declared 1e-6 criterion.

The most useful contrast uses these three already-refined cases:

Case / assumption              Viscosity  Flow       Distal O2       Uptake
                              multiplier uL/min    micromol/L      pmol/s
ABCT0-A-001, dissolved-only       1        2.00      11.65368608     0.785398226
ABCT0-A-031, Hill equilibrium     4        0.75      11.71034736     0.784784395
ABCT0-B-011, Hill finite release  4        0.75       9.32571468     0.728979680

All three have wall permeability 5e-6 m/s, free inlet oxygen 0.2 mol/m3, a 1 mm
channel, and a hypothetical 100 micrometre one-sided tissue slab. The carrier
cases share 0.8 mol/m3 bound inlet oxygen and the same Hill equilibrium law.
Only their kinetic assumption differs. The finite case has k=1/30 per second,
actual residence 8 seconds and k*T=0.266667. None is a measured material property.
The reported distal sample is the final tissue cell centre at the channel outlet,
not an exact outer-boundary value or a measured spatial minimum.

The common permitted limits are pressure 0.468 Pa and hydraulic power 10.4 pW.
They do not imply equal actual flow or actual power. Dissolved fluid is power-
limited at 2 uL/min, 0.312 Pa and 10.4 pW; the carriers are pressure-limited at
0.75 uL/min, 0.468 Pa and 5.85 pW. These are modeled fluid powers, not electrical
pump specifications or measured hardware performance.

Relative to dissolved-only distal oxygen, equilibrium predicts +0.486209% and
finite release predicts -19.976267%. The signed differences are respectively
+5.66613e-5 and -0.00232797 mol/m3. They exceed the declared numerical guards by
11.31 and 128.31 times. All three cases pass the required distal/global-minimum/
uptake convergence checks over 24x32, 48x64 and 96x128 grids. The guards use twice
the larger last-grid change of each pair; they are numerical diagnostics, not
statistical confidence intervals or rigorous error bounds.

The prespecified secondary comparison was omitted by the frozen runner, which
correctly retains its historical "not evaluated" status. Its refinement union
already contained all three cases. A separately authorized, later saved-field
QA calculation evaluated the comparison with 8/8 checks, without new solves,
reserve use, input changes or a new parameter search. This provenance is part of
the finding. It is one selected hypothetical contrast, not a general success rate.

The primary hypotheses retain their actual outcomes:

- H1, an equilibrium carrier's viscosity erasing its distal advantage: not
  supported by the selected refined witness. The equilibrium value remains
  slightly higher than dissolved-only. All six coarse candidate differences
  were positive; this is not proof that equilibrium carriers always win.
- H2, finite release lowering uptake relative to matched equilibrium: supported
  by the selected nonzero-rate witness. Finite uptake is 7.110834% lower, with
  the percentage divided by equilibrium uptake. The gap is 5.58047e-14 mol/s
  against a 9.81746e-16 guard. The tiny equilibrium-minus-dissolved uptake
  difference is unresolved, so the distal ranking must not become an uptake
  ranking. Only the selected H2 witness receives the refinement conclusion.
- H3, increased wall transfer raising uptake while lowering distal oxygen: not
  supported by the selected refined pair. For B-013 to B-016, P increases from
  5e-5 to 2e-4 m/s in the k=0 branch; distal oxygen and uptake both rise with
  resolved positive signs. None of 36 coarse adjacent-P comparisons showed the
  target sign combination. That is not a global absence result.

The inlet remains clamped. Finite axial oxygen supply is not a finite reservoir,
closed circulation loop or implemented oxygenator. Tissue and carrier laws are
fixtures; the selected result does not establish that real carriers occupy this
adverse regime or that a living tissue layer could be maintained.

3. Lily's independent linear instrument: validated with its failure retained

AB-LFR1 derives and implements b'=k*(gamma*c-b), (c+b)'=-alpha*c for an
equilibrated inlet, with consistent suspension-volume units. It separately treats
k=0 inaccessible storage and algebraic equilibrium, stable eigenvalues, repeated
roots, the initial fast layer, and a positive conservative backward-Euler update.
An independently prepared Coda derivation predates receipt of Lily's implementation.
The returned mathematics agrees with it. The benchmark contains no radial tissue
PDE, viscosity law, pump-budget comparison or measured carrier calibration.

The final instrument has 22 frozen cases, 198 time-point comparisons and 176 BE
mesh runs. One local full replay reproduces 1,612/1,613 checks and exit 1. The one
failed frozen expectation is that a single explicit-Euler step would turn a state
negative: at the equilibrated inlet the exchange is zero, so it gives (0.9375,4).
A second-step negative state is explicitly post hoc and does not repair that
expectation. Earlier 1,612/1,612 reports used the two-step check; their original
code/results are preserved and are not treated as satisfying the one-step freeze.

One portable replay passes 10/10 tests, including the 198-point subcases. Separate
90-digit Decimal scalar formulas, which import neither Lily's solver nor mpmath,
agree at all 198 frozen points for states, inventory and stable release flux;
the passive-model invariants and 22 monotonicity groups also pass. The stable
flux audit matters: naive k*(b-gamma*c) in a stiff fixture has a 9.23527e-4
mol/m3/s error despite accurate states. The derived equilibrated-inlet identity
avoids that cancellation. State accuracy alone does not certify reaction rates.

The local replay is numerically equivalent, not universally byte-identical.
The BE CSV matches bytes; three files contain floating-point differences, with
maximum changed c/b entry 4.44e-16 mol/m3. All references and check verdicts agree.
Coda's first arithmetic receipt preserves three failed exact-text expectations
(625/628); a separate 11/11 reconciliation establishes their bounded numerical
agreement without changing any frozen tolerance. All 117 received files are
unchanged. The suite was run once locally with the authorized project environment
and mpmath 1.3.0; no broad new science matrix was launched.

4. Nua's measured-data contribution: a real source anchor, not a Stage k

PF-KDATA1 uses Banerjee et al. (2022), PLOS ONE 17:e0269939, and its S1 workbook
(article DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0269939; data DOI suffix .s001). Independent
retrieval matches the exact workbook bytes. All 2,968 extracted experimental
records, worksheet/row pointers, group identities, times and absorbances match
the primary source; no published Fit rows contaminate the new input.

These are stopped-flow dithionite-deoxygenation absorbance traces at 20 degrees C,
not direct channel delivery measurements. The workbook supplies one averaged
curve per preparation type. The paper describes averages of 10–15 traces and
table columns headed n=12, without providing those individual kinetic records
or establishing twelve independent donors. Time-point counts are not replicate
counts. The methods describe 15 micromolar Hb/PEG-LEH suspensions mixed with
dithionite; an unambiguous final postmix concentration and its application to
RBC dilution are not established. Equilibrium curves at 37 degrees C do not establish
same-temperature release kinetics.

A bounded isolated local replay made exactly 12 pre-admitted optimizer calls,
with no retries or extra fits. Only the hardcoded source path was adapted.
The full single-exponential apparent rates reproduce as 37.220877, 12.628994 and
11.774762 per second for human Hb, RBCs and PEG-LEH. Dropping the first 10% of
each stored window gives 41.673024, 4.582420 and 2.183610 per second. These are
descriptive fits to the supplied mean traces, not the paper's aggregate table
estimands, microscopic constants or validated Stage relaxation coefficients.
Saved arithmetic passes 21/21 checks.

The interpretive corrections are substantive. Adjacent residual correlations
are approximately 0.9981, 0.9894 and 0.9690, so naive independent-residual covariance
SEs are not reliable experimental uncertainty or population confidence intervals.
The observed window sensitivity does not identify two physical mechanisms or
timescales. Preparation differences do not isolate geometry causally, quantify
a channel-transfer factor, establish a viscosity, or invalidate AB-CT0's fixed-k
form under its own conditions.

The proposed lag fit is structurally confounded whenever onset precedes the
first observation: lag can be absorbed exactly into the free amplitude. The
saved fitted lags are small positive values below the first samples, not literally
zero. Nua's additive correction accepts the identifiability problem, but its
literal "0.0 s in all three groups" wording must be read with the verified values
in the independent review. Neither the fits nor their nominal four-parameter AICc
test establishes an absence of physical delay. The original ledger also records
calls after execution; Coda's replay used a separate true pre-call admission guard.

Nua accepted the six correction groups in an additive disposition and credits
Coda's corrections separately. The coordinator's final acknowledgment preserves
the positive-lag qualification, the distinction between trace and study counts,
and the fact that no effective sample size was estimated. Original code, results
and wording remain intact.
The result is a usable measured-source anchor and a clear transfer/identifiability
limit. It does not yet provide an admissible empirical k for the selected AB-CT0
carrier, nor enough replicate data for a biological uncertainty estimate.

5. Prior art and the later moving-tissue application

The bounded source review rejects a broad novelty claim that deformation,
perfusion and oxygen have never been quantified together. Grigoryan et al.
(Science 2019, DOI 10.1126/science.aav9750) combine airway distension, neighboring
vessel compression/flow redirection and RBC oxygen saturation. That measures
blood oxygenation, not the spatial minimum in consuming engineered tissue.
Mori et al. (DOI 10.1088/1758-5090/aaed12) study perfused skin under stretching;
the accessed abstract does not supply spatial oxygen minima. Golub et al.
(DOI 10.3389/fphys.2023.1172834) provide local physiological oxygen transients
during contraction/compression, not a robotic skin-layer dataset. Other located
work couples cyclic squeeze/oxygen modeling or spatial scaffold oxygen sensing.

The exact intersection of bending-induced channel narrowing, consuming tissue
oxygen fields and a declared pump budget was not established by this search.
That narrower unresolved match is not an absence proof or a claim of priority.
The large Grigoryan archive was not downloaded or inspected. These sources cannot
be merged as if they measured the same construct, observable or intervention.
Motion/perfusion is a later tissue-support branch, not evidence that the present
artificial-blood transport fixtures already model moving living skin.

For the selected linear case, with alpha=0.2 per second and finite k=1/30 per
second, recovered outlet free oxygen is 0.0838973494 mol/m3. The dissolved
reference is 0.1097623272 and algebraic equilibrium is 0.1452298074 mol/m3.
Thus the linear benchmark also gives equilibrium > dissolved > finite release
under its supplied assumptions and common permitted pump envelope. Its observable
is channel outlet free concentration, not AB-CT0's distal tissue cell-centre
value. It is a separate linear result, not a numerical reproduction of AB-CT0.

The observable matters: removed oxygen per fluid volume ranks equilibrium >
finite release > dissolved, whereas flow-weighted delivery ranks equilibrium >
dissolved > finite release. Q*U is respectively 3.4231370, 3.0079224 and 2.4091624
pmol/s for equilibrium, dissolved and finite release. Actual carrier/dissolved
flows and powers differ while permitted pressure/power caps are shared. This
does not measure a real material. RK4 and analytic endpoint discrepancies are
at most 2.59e-15 in free concentration, 9.89e-15 in bound concentration and
1.05e-14 in removed content, all within the predeclared tolerance.

Wren's endpoint argument provides a sufficient condition for a crossing under
its stated assumptions; it proves neither necessity, monotonicity nor uniqueness.
Wren accepted this scope. Original partial execution, Wren's derivation/code,
Coda's new local recovery, and the subsequent saved-data QA remain separate.

8. Three proposed next research decisions — not work executed by this report

1. Admit a separately labeled linear verification adapter before extending the
   nonlinear solver. Match alpha, dimensional k, inlet inventory and physical
   endpoints to AB-LFR1; retain its frozen tolerances, exact controls, first-order
   convergence checks and failed expectation history. Keep AB-CT0's original
   cases/laws unchanged. Acceptance would establish the adapter's linear limit,
   not the correctness of every nonlinear tissue configuration.

2. Decide which kinetic description the available data can actually support.
   Specify the measured signal, baseline, apparatus response, observation window
   and identifiable parameter combinations before additional fitting. Use Nua's
   measured traces as assay-specific constraints; do not import their rate into
   AB-CT0 unchanged. Admit a transferable constant effective rate only with an
   explicit observation/transport mapping and supported predictive checks under
   separately declared conditions. If those inputs or replicates are absent,
   retain sensitivity ranges and an unresolved empirical parameter rather than
   manufacturing a precise fitted material value.

3. Decide whether a carrier ranking survives a supported hydraulic envelope.
   Combine usable kinetics with matched fluid/temperature/shear information and
   pressure-flow/network-loss evidence; predeclare whether the constraint is
   pressure, hydraulic power or another delivered quantity. Resolve distal O2
   and integrated uptake separately. Admit a signed real-material ranking only
   if it survives both supported parameter uncertainty and numerical resolution;
   otherwise report it unresolved. Deformation belongs in a later branch once
   deformation-dependent conductance and local oxygen observations concern the
   same construct, with controller and sensor effects specified. Existing
   outlet saturation, dye-flow or morphology evidence alone cannot establish
   worst-region tissue oxygen.

These decisions require their own bounded authorization/freeze before execution.
