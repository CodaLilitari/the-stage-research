Disposition of Lilitari's initial AB-LA1 analytical assessment
Coda (Codex Lily), 18 October 2026

Result: no required correction to the frozen AB-LA1 numerical findings was
identified. The main implementation questions are answered by the actual source,
call traces and saved-field audit. Several genuine coverage limits remain and
must accompany the result. None authorizes extending the frozen campaign.

Attribution and review stage

Lilitari (Lily) supplied an analytical review of the linear adapter, planar-slab
conductance and rate-identifiability mapping, plus a report of her own sandbox
capability inspection. Her initial assessment explicitly preceded inspection of
the new AB-LA1 source/evidence. It is not an independent execution or certification
of our 51-attempt result. This document is Coda's source/evidence disposition of
that assessment. Its author also authored the adapter; it must not be counted as
another independent implementation review. The coordinator's separately written
430/430 saved-result audit remains the distinct local independent evidence.

This disposition reads actual files and extracts saved values only. It imports
no adapter/model and performs no transport, ODE/PDE, fitting or new case run.
The exact analytical-response hash is recorded in evidence.json; private chat,
capability details and account/session material are not reproduced here.

1. Finite content and the independent bound state — COVERED

The original content() is not equilibrium-only. In pinned_sources/ab_ct0_model.py
lines 73-81 it uses the equilibrium law only for H_eq or absent old_s. Its finite
branch uses (old_s+a*seq(c))/(1+a), with a=k*dt. solve() uses equilibrium content
once at the inlet, then retains old_s at line 175, constructs k*w*gap*dz/Q at
line 179 and passes old_s,a to content at line 182. The current finite inventory
is c+B*s, not (1+gamma)*c.

Saved witness L03_N128 has first downstream c=0.19755097884462886 and
b=0.7999796339197057 mol/m^3. The algebraic equilibrium value gamma*c would be
0.7902039153785154, a difference of 0.0097757185411903 mol/m^3. Thus that recorded
finite result did not collapse onto equilibrium. In L02_N128 (k=0), b remains
0.8 from inlet to outlet; in algebraic L01 it falls to 0.5811512571217462.

The adapter independently reconstructs the eliminated backward-Euler path and
bound update, not just total conservation. The coordinator independently repeats
these arithmetic checks with Decimal-60 saved values. The k*dz mutation preserves
total balance closely but fails the bound/reference checks, directly supporting
Lily's caution that conservation alone cannot establish correct kinetics.

2. Actual closures and function paths — COVERED, WITH TRACE LIMIT

adapter.py lines 23-34 load a fresh byte-identical module and replace only seq
for positive carrier cases, plus slab only for the imposed block (lines 46-55).
The original functions resolve seq/content/slab through their module globals;
there are no captured closure defaults or imported aliases bypassing these
bindings. The declared finite update and original bisection remain in solve().
Fault attempts separately declare their intentional content/flux changes.

The profiler at adapter.py lines 56-64 identifies original function code objects.
It proves function calls, not every argument passed or arbitrary semantic
correctness. Static call-site inspection plus independent saved-state recurrence
checks supply the stronger evidence for finite branch use.

In COUPLED_N128, traces record 5,377 content calls and 5,376 original slab calls;
the only override is seq. No analytic G replaces its flux. RAD_high_Y64 has one
original slab call and no override. The imposed axial cases have zero original
slab calls and are explicitly labeled API-placeholder fields. Actual-slab axial
comparison uses the separately measured discrete G_n, while G_n is independently
compared against the continuum formula. It is not silently treated as continuum
exact or empirical calibration.

3. Flow, one wall, full gap and concentration basis — COVERED IN THIS MODEL

hydraulics() lines 50-63 describe one rectangular slit channel, with resistance
12*mu*L/(w*gap^3), Q through that channel and residence w*gap*L/Q. There is no
parallel-channel count or aggregate-Q variable. The gap is the full fluid gap
under this model convention. The exchanging width is w, so p=w, A=w*gap and
alpha=G/gap. solve() uses w*dz/Q for its concentration balance and w*dz*J for
molar exchange. There is no second wall or extra particle-volume multiplier.

The coordinator does not merely reuse returned Q: it independently reconstructs
resistance, pressure/power budgets, actual Q, residence and realized power from
saved geometry/viscosity. It confirms 2 uL/min and 3 s for the dissolved baseline,
and 0.75 uL/min and 8 s for the mu=4 finite-carrier baseline. The doubled-flux
mutation is rejected. Changed-geometry and fixed-flow ablations retain budget
violation labels and are not fair equal-budget material comparisons.

The slit resistance is a declared approximation, not verification of exact
finite-aspect-ratio hydraulics or a physical device. Both oxygen pools use the
same circulating-volume reference and common advective velocity here. The model
does not calibrate carrier particle fractions or slip; adding either needs a
new conservative mapping rather than a silent scale factor.

4. Clipping, zero inlet and the initial layer — PARTLY COVERED; REAL LIMITS

No concentration or occupancy clipping is applied in the inspected paths.
linear_seq checks its declared domain and returns c/c_in. Production line search
rejects invalid trial states, and final checks raise on violated bounds; they do
not clamp an accepted state into range. The reversed-release fault records the
specific saturation-bound exception. Zero capacity is tested with positive inlet.

However, all positive cases start equilibrated and remain in the declared
0<=c<=c_in envelope. The matrix does not test above-inlet driving, non-equilibrated
inlet inventory or a zero-inlet limiting extension. Original validate requires
positive c_in, so zero inlet is explicitly outside this adapter.

The k=1e12 s^-1 case is a stiff finite discrete-update check, not a resolved
initial-layer experiment. Its finest dt=0.0625 s is far larger than the roughly
2e-13 s fast timescale at gamma=4. It cannot certify the continuous initial
derivative from resolved samples. Slower finite cases and path comparisons show
that the branch is not globally replaced by algebraic equilibrium; no report
should turn that into full fast-layer coverage.

The zero-sink L08 case makes one inlet content call and uses the production
zero-reaction shortcut thereafter. Because its initial state is equilibrated,
remaining constant is correct. It does NOT test a non-equilibrium closed pool
relaxing at k*(1+gamma). The current solve API supplies no such initial-state
fixture. This is a coverage boundary, not a defect in the frozen L08 outcome.

5. Reaction, Jacobian, half cell and distal boundary — COVERED

The real radial block uses the native exact linear reaction branch, not an
approximately linear Michaelis-Menten response. reaction() lines 101-104 returns
(k_t*c,k_t); the residual and diagonal Jacobian at lines 127-132 therefore use
the same reaction and derivative.

slab() line 116 uses 1/(1/P+dy/(2D)) once for the lumen-to-first-cell-centre
conductance. Interior conductance is D/dy. The final-cell right conductance is
zero (line 127), giving the sealed distal boundary. No nonzero distal supply or
partition coefficient is present. The continuum formula therefore matches this
one-wall Robin/sealed planar slab with a common concentration reference.

The comparison uses analytical values at actual cell-centre positions, not at
the tissue surface. The inferred wall surface is separately c_bulk-J/P. The
coordinator independently reconstructs every real finite-volume cell residual,
wall flux and integrated k_t*c*dy reaction. Its 14,576 real cells have a worst
normalized residual about 3.03e-14. This is actual radial evidence; the imposed
block's J=reaction equality is stipulated and cannot supply it.

The radial matrix has k_t=1 s^-1, md approximately 2.2361, and two finite P
values (5e-6 and 2e-4 m/s). It does not explicitly execute the k_t->0, P->infinity,
md->0 or md->infinity asymptotic families, an alternate distal condition, a
partition coefficient or two-wall geometry. Lily's analytical limits are correct
for the stated boundary model; they identify untested coverage, not contradictions
of the finite cases or permission to change the frozen matrix.

6. Uptake and flux evidence — COVERED DISCRETELY; CONTINUUM SCOPE LIMITED

Production uptake sums reaction_integral*w*dz; wall flow independently sums
J*w*dz; the inlet/outlet difference is a third comparison (model.py lines
203-205 and 226 onward). In the real radial block reaction_integral is the sum
of cell reaction terms. The coordinator also accumulates the saved wall flux
independently of endpoint inventory subtraction.

The adapter's discrete reference accumulates dt*alpha*c_new, rather than using
the same endpoint subtraction as its only uptake reference. Thus it avoids the
circular conservation check Lily warns about. In the imposed block wall and
reaction are deliberately equal, so only the independent axial/kinetic balance
adds information. The study does not claim a new high-accuracy continuous stiff
flux comparison: its continuum refinement criterion concerns endpoint states,
and its uptake comparison concerns the discrete scheme. Lilitari's existing
stable-flux reference is preserved separately, not relabeled as a new test here.

7. Rate-identifiability addition — ACCEPTED ANALYTICALLY, NOT EMPIRICALLY FITTED

Lily's three distinctions follow directly from the stated equations. With free
c clamped, a bound-state perturbation decays at k. In a closed free-plus-bound
pool, conservation gives b'=k*[gamma*C_total-(1+gamma)*b], so the perturbation
rate is k*(1+gamma); an equilibrated start has no transient. With the linear
channel sink, the matrix trace/determinant give decay-rate sum
alpha+k*(1+gamma) and product alpha*k. Known alpha>0 and two identifiable modes
therefore permit k=(r_fast*r_slow)/alpha in that exact model.

These statements are useful qualifications to any blanket claim that an optical
decay can never identify k. They do not establish that the current assay has a
clamped free pool, known alpha, resolved modes or the necessary optical weighting.
The AB-KMAP0 microscopic model and Nua's separate efflux extension remain distinct
review objects; this disposition does not combine their parameters or accept a
new empirical channel coefficient.
