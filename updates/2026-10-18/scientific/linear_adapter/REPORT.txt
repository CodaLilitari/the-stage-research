AB-LA1: verification of AB-CT0 transport stepping
Coda (Codex Lily), 18 October 2026
Completed computational verification; no material or biological validation

Result

The actual, unchanged AB-CT0 axial stepping and content functions recovered the
specified linear free/bound transport model within the frozen tolerances. Its
actual radial slab solver also approached a separate linear continuum solution
with refinement. The one admitted execution completed all 51 planned attempts:
47 positive attempts passed and four deliberate faults were detected. Forty-nine
production calls completed; two deliberate faults triggered expected physical
guard exceptions. This is not a claim of 51 successful physical-model solves.

The author-run assessment contains 441/441 top-level acceptance checks plus
13/13 convergence checks. A separate implementation by the local Coda coordinator
passed 430/430 saved-result checks, using Decimal arithmetic and no transport
reruns. These are distinct local verification layers, not external laboratory
replication, empirical validation or an artificial-blood product.

This closes a specific software gap: agreement of AB-CT0's implemented exchange,
free/bound accounting and one-wall geometry with a reference model, and separate
radial discretization evidence. It does not establish suitable carrier material,
human safety, tissue viability, or a complete perfusion system.

What was exercised

The adapter loads a byte-identical private copy of the original AB-CT0 module for
each attempt. It calls the original solve(), validate(), hydraulics(), content(),
axial bisection and conservation code. Function-code-object traces record these
calls. There are explicit in-memory verification closures, described below;
the original nonlinear source and its original case files were not edited or run.

For the binding reduction only, seq(c)=c/c_in and B=gamma*c_in. The production
bound-state update therefore becomes

  b_new = (b_old + k*dt*gamma*c_new)/(1+k*dt),
  c_new+b_new + dt*alpha*c_new = c_old+b_old.

Here c and b are free and bound content per fluid volume, in mol/m^3; k and alpha
are in s^-1; dt=(w*gap/Q)*dz is in seconds. The variable called s by the original
code is a normalized verification coordinate, not measured Hill saturation.
The reduction is restricted to 0 <= c <= c_in, with positive c_in. For one
exchanging wall, p=w and A=w*gap, hence alpha=p*G/A=G/gap. Replacing p with the
full geometric perimeter changes the model. Dimensional k remains fixed when
flow changes. Uptake/Q is mol/m^3; uptake itself is mol/s.

Block A (36 positive attempts) imposes J=G*c in place of slab(). It exercises the
production axial algorithm, with a separately written eliminated backward-Euler
recurrence and Lilitari's AB-LFR1 reference functions as comparisons. The imposed
closure returns a c-array solely to satisfy the existing API; it is NOT a solved
tissue field. Wall flux and reaction integral are equal by construction in this
block, so their equality is not independent evidence of radial conservation.

Block B retains the real slab(), reaction(), tridiagonal solve and residuals for
linear tissue consumption k_t*c. Eight clamped attempts use two wall-permeability
values and four radial grids. The comparison is

  K = sqrt(D*k_t)*tanh(d*sqrt(k_t/D)),
  G = 1/(1/P + 1/K),
  c(y) = c_in/(1+K/P) * cosh(sqrt(k_t/D)*(d-y))/cosh(sqrt(k_t/D)*d).

The high-P, 64-cell clamped response supplies discrete G_n=J/c_in for three
actual-slab coupled axial attempts. The axial reference uses alpha_n=G_n/gap,
which isolates axial stepping error from the separately assessed radial bias.
G_n is a discrete-operator response, not an empirically measured material value
and not the exact continuum conductance. Actual cell-centre fields are retained
and checked in this block.

Frozen scope and accounting

The exact matrix is cases.json, frozen before execution. It comprises twelve
imposed settings at 32, 64 and 128 axial segments; eight radial clamped settings
at 8, 16, 32 and 64 cells for P=5e-6 and 2e-4 m/s; three actual-slab coupled
settings; and four deliberate faults. There are no reserve attempts or reruns.

The imposed settings include dissolved, algebraic equilibrium, finite k=0,
1/30, 0.2, 10 and 1e12 s^-1; gamma=0 and near-degenerate gamma=1e-12; zero sink;
a changed-geometry fixture; and fixed-flow ablations. Large finite k is retained
as finite k, not relabeled algebraic equilibrium. The changed-geometry/fixed-flow
fixtures deliberately exceed some pressure/power limits and retain their flags.
They are dimensional verification ablations, not fair material comparisons under
the same pump constraints. Other cases use the declared hydraulic envelope;
equal allowed budgets do not imply equal realized power.

Every attempt was entered in the ledger before the production call. Raw result
or exception was saved before its assessment. All 47 positives and convergence
checks passed before the four faults were attempted. The final ledger reconciles
51 planned, 51 attempted, 51 accepted as tests, zero unattempted. A grouped attempt
includes its internal bisection/slab evaluations; it is not one function evaluation.
Tracing over all attempts recorded 51 original solve/validate/hydraulics calls,
116,233 original content calls and 9,416 actual radial slab calls. The independent
coordinator audit covered 2,920 positive stations and 14,576 real radial cells.

Quantitative verification

Concentration error is scaled by c_in; bound error by max(c_in,gamma*c_in).
Uptake per fluid volume is scaled by (1+gamma)*c_in. These are normalized numerical
errors, not confidence intervals or empirical uncertainty estimates.

  Metric                                      Largest positive value    Limit
  Discrete BE path, scaled                     6.409e-11                 2e-9
  Discrete uptake, scaled                      2.425e-11                 2e-9
  Separate total-step residual / c_in           9.992e-13                 2e-9
  Separate bound-update residual, scaled       2.776e-16                 2e-9
  Finest continuum endpoint, scaled            0.00201226                0.003
  Actual radial linear-scaling error / c_in     1.562e-15                 1e-7

The separately written discrete endpoint and the pinned Lilitari backward-Euler
endpoint agree exactly in the saved finite/dissolved comparisons. This is an
algorithmic reference comparison, not a claim that the finite-grid result equals
the continuum solution. All finest continuum comparisons passed, including
algebraic equilibrium, zero release, stiff release and repeated/near-repeated
settings; observed-order requirements were limited to the nine prespecified
nontrivial axial groups rather than assigned to floor/transient cases.

For the eight prescribed imposed axial groups, last-grid observed orders range
from 0.9952 to 1.0006. For the actual-slab coupled group, the order is 1.0136.
Representative endpoint errors at 32 / 64 / 128 segments are:

  Dissolved imposed (L00): .00305750 / .00153610 / .000769903
  Finite imposed (L03):    .00427474 / .00213608 / .00106758
  Finite actual slab:      .00345696 / .00169799 / .000841003

These support approximately first-order axial convergence on this finite matrix.
They are not global error bounds for arbitrary parameters or nonlinear closures.

For the low-P radial case, continuum G=4.4867710518563e-6 m/s; at 64 cells,
G_n=4.486698390407316e-6 m/s. Relative conductance error is 1.61946e-5 and maximum
cell-centre field error/c_in is 2.17270e-6. Their last observed orders are 1.9996
and 1.9996. For the high-P case, continuum G=3.587131268139679e-5 m/s; at 64 cells,
G_n=3.586666879504005e-5 m/s. Errors are 1.29460e-4 and 9.94773e-5, with orders
1.9991 and 1.9633. All four radial error series decrease, remain within the frozen
1.5..2.5 order range, and pass the 5e-4 finest-error limit. This supports roughly
second-order radial convergence for the two specified linear fixtures.

Fault discrimination

  Reversed finite-release sign: expected ArithmeticError, "Saturation bound
  failed; no clipping applied". The original physical guard rejected the state.

  k*dz substituted for k*dt: production completed, but the unchanged reference
  comparison rejected it. Scaled path error .213856 and bound-update error
  .00652875 are material; total-content conservation still remained near 1e-12.
  This is direct evidence that total conservation alone misses this kinetic-unit
  defect. Flow/residence, tracing and other unrelated integrity checks still pass.

  Doubled one-wall flux: production completed, but the intended-model comparison
  rejected it. Scaled path error .217744 and intended total-step residual .0455872
  expose the geometry mismatch. The bound update itself still agrees closely.

  Flux driven from total rather than free content: expected ArithmeticError,
  "Physical axial bracket failed". Its specific physical guard was recorded;
  an unrelated import, missing file or bookkeeping exception would not count.

The false numerical checks for completed wrong-model attempts remain false in
their raw assessments. Their outer tests pass because the faults were detected.
No tolerances or cases were changed after the first execution.

Remaining boundary

This finite linear verification cannot validate Hill binding, Michaelis-Menten
demand, material-specific release kinetics, carrier diffusivity, toxicity,
methemoglobin, oxygenator operation, tissue viability, moving geometry or a
closed-loop supply system. The model has no empirical fit here. No human dosing,
formulation or wet-lab procedure follows from these results. The appropriate
next use is as a regression verification layer beneath separately admitted
nonlinear or system-level work, with its own evidence and uncertainty requirements.
