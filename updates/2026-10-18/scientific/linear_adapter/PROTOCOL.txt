Architecture and exact boundary
Load a byte-identical pinned copy of AB-CT0 model.py into a fresh private module
for EACH attempt. Call its unchanged solve(), validate(), hydraulics(), content()
and axial bisection. No nonlinear source or original case is edited or executed.
For carrier verification ONLY, replace module.seq(c,cfg) with c/c_in and set
B=gamma*c_in. Thus s is a bounded normalized verification coordinate, not Hill
saturation; b=B*s gives b_eq=gamma*c and the production finite update gives
b_new=(b_old+k*dt*gamma*c_new)/(1+k*dt). The declared envelope has 0<=c<=c_in.
Zero inlet is excluded because the production validator requires positive cin.

Block A replaces slab() with the IMPOSED closure J=G*c and integral=J. Its c-array
is only an API placeholder, explicitly not a tissue profile. This does not test
the radial PDE or independently test wall-versus-reaction equality: that equality
is imposed. It DOES exercise the production axial loop, content update, residence
conversion, one-wall width factor, bracket solve, inventory caps and saved balances.
alpha=p*G/A=G/gap for one exchanging wall, p=w and A=w*gap. A full geometric
perimeter must not be substituted. A fixed dimensional k stays fixed when Q changes.

Block B keeps the REAL slab(), reaction(), tridiagonal solve and residuals with
linear tissue consumption kt*c. Eight clamped solves (two P values, four radial
grids) compare G_n=J/c_in and every cell-centre field with the continuum:
K=sqrt(D*kt)*tanh(d*sqrt(kt/D)); G=1/(1/P+1/K);
c(y)=c_in/(1+K/P)*cosh(sqrt(kt/D)*(d-y))/cosh(sqrt(kt/D)*d).
The high-P, ny=64 clamped result supplies G_n for three coupled axial refinements.
Those use the REAL slab plus linear binding closure. Comparison with alpha_n=G_n/
gap isolates axial BE error from radial discretization. G_n is a measured discrete
operator response, not an independent empirical calibration. Its independent
continuum/grid check is separate. Real coupled fields also check linear scaling
against the saved clamped field. No radial result is silently called continuum exact.

Anticipated failure modes and limits
Potential failures include a changed source binding, wrong effective perimeter,
k in inverse seconds inserted into a spatial step without A/Q, a free/bound sign
error, finite inventory treated as instantly accessible, sampling at wrong axial
coordinates, conflating imposed flux with solved tissue, or double-counting a
capacity multiplier. The tests explicitly separate continuum discretization
error from discrete-solver error and preserve exact versus finite equilibrium.
The imposed block cannot validate radial physiology, and neither block validates
the nonlinear Hill/MM regime, material kinetics, moving geometry, an oxygenator,
closed-loop operation, tissue viability or clinical use. Local author-run checks
are not external validation; independent admission and saved-result QA remain
separate responsibilities.
