Actual results and failure accounting

eight BE meshes per case (176 mesh runs). Its final runner has 1,613 assertions:

negative component. This follows directly from the independently derived initial
derivatives. The second-step negative state is a post-hoc control, not repair of

failure report, and output. Exit 1 is retained and was reproduced locally.

One standard-library portable replay passed 10/10 tests (including the 198-point
state/rate subcases). One full replay reproduced 1,612/1,613 and exactly the same
failed assertion. No test or benchmark rerun was performed to obtain a pass.
Commands are preserved in execution_receipt.json and console files. Execution
used project Python 3.12.14 on Windows, mpmath 1.3.0 installed by the coordinator
from the official wheel. The independent reviewer did not install or download
anything. The wrapper pinned itself to one available logical CPU, inherited by
children, and imposed a 300-second total subprocess timeout. Portable command
elapsed 0.156 seconds; full command 1.359 seconds, benchmark loop 1.215 seconds.
Local peak memory was not instrumented; Lily's sandbox memory remains her
reported measurement. No network, GPU, case extension or tissue PDE solve occurred.

The returned derivation agrees on the following substantive points:

- c and b share units of oxygen per circulating suspension volume. The exchange
  has opposite signs and cancels exactly in total inventory; the sink is alpha*c.
  The conversions alpha=p*G/A and T=A*L/Q are dimensionally correct, and p counts
  only exchanging walls. G is an effective linear conductance, not automatically
  a measured raw wall permeability.
- Trace/determinant and scalar equation are correct. The product formula for the
  slow eigenvalue avoids cancellation. Lily's h=alpha+k*gamma-k representation
  yields the same discriminant as the independent (alpha-k*(1+gamma))^2+4*alpha*k*gamma.
  Matrix diagonal weights differ from equilibrated-state mixture weights; the
  implementation applies them as a propagator correctly.
- Exact repeated roots use the Jordan off-diagonal term. Near-coincident roots
  use expm1/divided differences; no epsilon denominator or clipping substitutes
  for the model. Zero demand/capacity/rate/inlet and long-transit underflow have
  explicit interpretations within the declared envelope.
- k=0 keeps initially bound inventory inaccessible. Algebraic equilibrium is a
  separate model, not a large finite-k label. The derivative mismatch at t=0 is
  correctly preserved even though states approach equilibrium at large k.
- Conservative backward Euler follows elimination of the two implicit balances.
  Its sink and transfer are independently accumulated; the stable eliminated
  transfer avoids subtracting two stiff, nearly equal final-state quantities.
- For equilibrated inlet, release=alpha*gamma*c0*E12 is correct: differentiating
  b=gamma*c0*(exp(-rs*t)+rs*(exp(-rs*t)-exp(-rf*t))/(rf-rs)) gives that identity.
  It is correctly restricted to this inlet law. Q*U, not U alone, is mol/s.

Next-use boundary

The benchmark can supply an independent linear verification mode for a later
adapter. That adapter must preserve dimensional k, match physical endpoints and
exchange perimeter, separate axial error from any radial reduction, and keep the
original nonlinear AB-CT0 cases intact. No adapter was built or run here. Neither
Lily's instrument nor this local audit evaluated a viscosity law, pump budget,
recirculating reservoir, oxygenator, measured biological parameters or clinical
performance. The pre-return fixed-budget rank-reversal argument remains a
separate conditional mathematical result, not a result of this benchmark suite.

No further required source correction was identified in this bounded review.
Any report of these results must retain the one frozen-control failure, the
post-hoc status of its extension/flux audit, and the cross-platform distinction.
