    conversion. a,k have inverse-time units; c,b are contents in the same units.

Closed-form numerical implementation

[ ] Verify trace and determinant, root ordering and nonnegative solution weights.
[ ] Treat exactly zero demand/rate/storage and repeated roots explicitly.
