## An actionable calibration sequence using existing public material

1. **Start with the observation equation, not a rate label.** Preserve measured absorbance/fluorescence, the unknown gain, time window, baseline and the documented forcing proxy separately. Do not merge fluorescence ratios with absorbance as though they were calibrated concentrations.
2. **Conditional chemistry calibration.** The paired free-Hb/liposome study offers a concrete published route for constraining a chemical coefficient before an exchange coefficient. Its assumptions must be carried into the uncertainty set. Free and encapsulated material must be compatible before equating their chemical coefficients; cross-paper temperatures, concentration references and Hb state are not interchangeable.
3. **A discrimination test, not a pooled fit.** Ask whether a fixed-chemistry, variable-exchange model can explain a documented set of conditions. In the local model, shared chemistry requires `Delta D=d*Delta T` and, when calibrated, constant `N/D`. A failed relation rejects that sharing assumption or the local model. Current PF-KDATA1 full/late descriptive rates are not `T,D` and cannot perform this test.
4. **Held-out forcing/geometry information.** The single-cell study establishes that forcing and geometry can be observed rather than absorbed into one fitted exponential. Its published contrasts can challenge a proposed universal time constant. It is a model-discrimination route, not permission to borrow its reported rate as a PEG-LEH parameter.
5. **Stop honestly at the public-data boundary.** No selected newly inspected source supplied a ready numeric, paired-input/replicate dataset that uniquely separates all chemistry, membrane and film parameters. The next admissible numerical work is the synthetic identifiability diagnostic. A later empirical calibration would require an explicitly admitted figure-level extraction with extraction uncertainty, or further authorized public-data retrieval. No author contact or new biological experiment is proposed here.

## Publication boundary

Public candidate files are the authored derivation, protocol, constraint table, source summary/receipt, analytic diagnostic source/config/expected results and their execution receipt if admitted. Do not include local article XML/HTML, source figures, a full paper, private reviewer conversations or local absolute paths by default. If later reusing a source figure or data extraction, credit the actual authors, DOI and CC BY 4.0, link the license and identify modifications. The authored diagnostic is synthetic; external papers are evidence inputs, not authors of its implementation.
