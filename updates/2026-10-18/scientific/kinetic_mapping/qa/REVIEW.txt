Independent state-space result

Write x=(c,b), A=[[-q-a,d],[a,-d]], B=[q,0]^T and C=[0,1]. Exact matrix trace,
determinant and C*A*B give T=q+a+d, D=q*d and N=a*q. Cayley-Hamilton yields
b''+T*b'+D*b=N*u because C*B=0. This independently checks the sign, units and
input numerator in DERIVATION.md section 2. T has units s^-1; D,N have s^-2.

For t=a+d, the partner is (q',a',d')=(t,a*q/t,d*q/t). The explicit invertible
state transform is

  S = [[t/q, d*(q-t)/(a*q)], [0,1]].

Then A'*S=S*A, B'=S*B and C*S=C. These are stronger checks than merely observing
matching poles at sampled times. They establish the same zero-state input/output
map for every admissible waveform in this linear model. In C04, the matrix is
S=[[4,-27/7],[0,1]], and the exact numerical matrix identities all hold.

The transform also fixes the equilibrium initial line: S*[c,(a/d)*c]^T is that
same vector. Thus the recorded release from an initially equilibrated state does
not by itself break the ambiguity. For arbitrary known initial states, equality
requires x'_0=S*x_0; it must not be inferred merely from identical b_0. The
transformation is not an unconditional map of every physically admissible
nonlinear state, and the derivation correctly remains a local-deviation claim.

There are generally two distinct positive parameter vectors, but the roots
coincide when q=a+d. The quadratic discriminant for recovering q is
T^2-4(D+N)=(q-a-d)^2. At coincidence the partner equals the original and S=I.
This does not contradict the strictly distinct decay poles for a,d,q>0: their
different discriminant is T^2-4D=(q-d)^2+a^2+2a(q+d)>0.

For the recorded pair and matched b_0=1,b'_0=0, both c_0 values are 9/7, but
c'_0=-18/7 versus -72/7. An informative internal-free transient distinguishes
this pair. It would not do so in an unperturbed equilibrium; neither does the
coincident parameter case provide two different mechanisms to distinguish.

Other checked conclusions

The three free-decay settings share T=10,D=9 and poles 1,9 s^-1, with output
(9*exp(-t)-exp(-9*t))/8. Their N values differ (7,12,15), demonstrating why
identical free decay with matched initial outputs is not identical calibrated
driven response. The negative fast-mode coefficient is real; an assumed positive
sum of exponentials would exclude this valid equilibrium-release response.

Shared chemistry C06 recovers a=7/2,d=9/2,q_1=2,q_2=5 exactly from the recorded
coefficient differences, and the calibrated N/D ratios also agree. C07 has
Delta T=0, Delta D=6, which contradicts shared d in this model. These are exact
synthetic tests. They do not establish that two physical preparations share
chemistry, that poles can be estimated robustly, or that practical fitting is
unique in the presence of noise and uncertain forcing.

The two membrane/film pairs yield the same K=1 micrometre/s and q=3 s^-1 for
the declared area/volume of 3 inverse micrometres. This does not support attributing
the whole exchange resistance to the membrane.

The internal-free QSSA is the Schur complement of the c row, giving forward
a*q/(q+a), reverse d*q/(q+a), and equilibrium ratio a/d. Independent high-precision
pole arithmetic confirms the valid-example relative slow-rate error near
1.571053e-5 and the invalid-example error 7/11=0.6363636. Fast chemistry is a
different limit: C11 has exact slow pole near 0.09991007195 s^-1, chemistry-fast
approximation 0.1 s^-1 (relative error about 0.00090009), and internal-free QSSA
relative error about 0.1108769. No favorable isolated comparison proves the
approximation for every forcing or initial condition.

For the low-frequency alternative, D/T=lambda_s*lambda_f/(lambda_s+lambda_f),
so its relative difference from lambda_s is exactly
-lambda_s/(lambda_s+lambda_f). This verifies the stated O(lambda_s/lambda_f)
claim, including its sign. The two controlled reductions need the explicit
forcing-frequency and initial-layer qualifications already stated in section 4.

The nonlinear zero-input scaling check confirms R=13, total'=-4 and scaled
values 26,-8 for eta=2, with unchanged optical output. With the same nonzero
external input, the vector-field scaling defect is q*(1-eta)*u, equal to -2*u
in this case. This establishes the specific zero-input symmetry; it does not
prove that every calibrated nonlinear experiment remains ambiguous.

Qualifications for reuse (not failures of the frozen examples)

1. DERIVATION.md lines 65-69: retain the word "generally" and add the coincident
   exception q=a+d when summarizing the twofold ambiguity. Qualify "Measuring
   internal free c(t) would distinguish the partners" as an informative transient
   for distinct partners. A constant equilibrium is a simple counterexample to
   an unconditional measurement-sufficiency claim. The current recorded release
   example is informative and passes.

2. analytic_diagnostic.py lines 50-52 implement only the declared rejection
   witness Delta T=0,Delta D!=0. The expression called shared_chemistry_consistent
   there is not a complete general consistency validator. Do not expose it as
   one for arbitrary new inputs; a full checker also needs positivity and the
   recovered common a/d/numerator conditions described in the derivation. No
   new diagnostic inputs were executed in this review.

3. The runner writes a pre-call ledger, then updates that same record to its
   final status (lines 185-196). The code supports the stated ordering, but the
   surviving final ledger has no per-attempt timestamp or immutable sequence
   of earlier states. The receipt therefore does not independently certify the
   temporal pre-call persistence of every attempt. Resource checks are cooperative
   before/after small analytic calculations, as README.md line 11 says; they
   are not OS-enforced quotas. These limits do not undermine the completed
   small algebra calculation or justify a claim of hard enforcement.
