Primary questions: what the saved results actually establish

Not supported by the selected refined witness in this matrix. All six coarse
prespecified H1 contrasts were positive; the smallest was selected and refined.
At P=5e-6 m/s, dissolved-only viscosity 1 at Q=2 uL/min (ABCT0-A-001) has fine-grid
distal tissue oxygen 0.011653686081216075 mol/m3. Hill equilibrium viscosity 4 at
Q=0.75 uL/min (ABCT0-A-031) has 0.011710347359284015 mol/m3. The carrier-minus-
dissolved difference is +5.666127806793972e-5 mol/m3, greater than its numerical
guard 5.011502687085612e-6. It is a small positive difference, approximately
0.4862% of the dissolved value, not the prespecified zero/negative counterexample.
This does not prove equilibrium carrier storage always wins outside the matrix,
nor certify the same numerical resolution for its unrefined cases. Both profiles
obey the same pressure/power limits; their actual flows differ by viscosity.

gap by EQUILIBRIUM uptake, not finite uptake. The numerical guard is
