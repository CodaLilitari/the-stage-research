Scientific expectations and denominator corrections

E5: The nonlinear 'most limited first' series heuristic is not always optimal:
68/2,520 configurations favor another order. The strongest saved example is the
two-sided hotspot, demand ratio 4, original depth 200 um, wall P 0.2 mm/s, beta 1,
total Q 1 uL/min. Heuristic HSa>HSb>B1>B2>B3 yields 0.00419497329195 mol/m3;
best B1>B2>HSa>HSb>B3 yields 0.00507266027506. The best value is 20.9223% greater
than the heuristic; the heuristic is 17.3023% below the best. Report line 149's
'won by up to 17.3%' needs its denominator made explicit. No data cell is wrong.

E6: Two-sided beta 1 beats base beta 256 in 102/119 declared geometry comparisons,
and reaches the reference in 67/119; 17 exceptions remain. For demand ratio 4,
original depth 200 um, P 0.2 mm/s, Q 1 uL/min, two-sided beta 1 max-min is
0.00309080937127, versus base beta 256 at 0.0462698160593 mol/m3. This directly defeats
an unconditional claim that halving depth always beats increasing capacity.
Two-sided supply retains tissue volume but adds a fifth channel and exchange
access: it is not a fixed-hardware comparison.

Equal-flow/equal-power distinction: max-min crosses the reference when uniform
split fails in 67 configurations across all beta at equal total flow; 38 of these
are beta 1. Under equal pump power, beta 1 only, there are 20 crossings. Report
line 157 follows an equal-power paragraph, so its 67 must be labeled as equal-flow
across all beta. The independently recounted E7 comparison remains 504/504:
equal-power max-min is at least the heuristic series result in that tested grid.
The main flow/pressure/power CSV columns describe the equal-flow state; the final
equal-power oxygen column is evaluated at different flows.

Median max-min-over-uniform gain is 30.6574% in the declared flow-limited bin
(668 configurations) and 0.51015% near the transverse ceiling (1,642). Thus
'no improvement helps near the ceiling' is too absolute. The PF0 pass count is
55/144, split 16/48, 19/48, 20/48 for beta 1, 4, 16; no classification flips occurred.

Static and mathematical review, separately attributed

architecture_review/review.txt contains 13/13 static/rational evidence checks by
the sibling project reviewer, including primary-source attribution. That reviewer
did not run transport solves; these are not additional numerical test cases.
The core model equations and the zero-order/first-order sorting arguments were
accepted. The actionable corrections are:

1. Bonart's paper explicitly discusses Graetz number after Eq 4. The reduction
   omits explicit radial resistance; the paper does not omit the condition.
2. Small illustrative channel pressure does not establish that pump cost matters
   only at body scale. A power/pressure budget, efficiency and omitted network
   losses are required before that conclusion.
3. Two flows and free-O2 profiles do not automatically identify q, D, P. If beta is
   unknown, scaling (q, D, P, beta) together leaves every free-O2 field unchanged.
   A constant film closure identifies effective conductance, not its two
   component resistances separately. This is a substantive inference limitation.
4. stage_transport.py's max-min docstring has an extra beta; the implementation
   is correct. Do not change numerical outputs to match the docstring error.
5. The nonlinear P*=0.0140/0.0139 mm/s values are three-point quadratic estimates
   around a 13-point scan, not directly solved continuous optima.
6. 'Topology matters more than split' is not supported by the displayed example.
   Retain the scoped equal-flow ordering result, its equal-power qualification,
   and the nonlinear failures.
7. Equal-power convergence/achieved-power certificates should become explicit
   before extending the solver. Saved maximum fixed-point residual 9.7233e-13
   meets its 1e-12 target, so this is not an observed failure of the current grid.

The substantive boundary is a steady independent-slab mathematical screen with
liver-like illustrative constants and hypothetical linear Ctotal=beta*Cfree.
It neither validates living skin nor a carrier, actual pump, circulation system
or clinical use. Retain Red's work as a useful reproduced mathematical baseline,
attach the corrections above, and keep empirical validity distinct from code QA.
