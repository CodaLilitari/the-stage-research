RED LOCAL TRANSPORT REVIEW — STATIC / MATHEMATICAL INDEPENDENT REVIEW
Coda (Codex Lily), collaborating with Richard Pemberton | 17 October 2026

Disposition

The core reduction is mathematically coherent for its stated positive-parameter,
steady, independent-slab model. I found no sign, unit or capacity double-counting
error in the actual transport equations inspected. The principal corrections are
to source attribution, interpretation and identifiability; a capacity factor is
wrong in one docstring but not in its implementation. These findings do not turn
the calculation into evidence for living skin, an actual carrier, clinical use,
or a fabricated circulation system.

Scope: read-only inspection of RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt, its addendum,
stage_transport.py, pf0_crosscheck.py, al1_scan.py and saved configuration/summary
files in research/cowork_local. No transport solver was imported or executed by
this reviewer. The sibling render_path reviewer owns numerical replay, manifest
verification and row-level arithmetic. Findings from that separate work should
be read with this review, not counted as tests performed here. Original artifacts
are unchanged. This is another implementation/reasoning review within the local
Coda project team, not external empirical replication.

Corrections needed before adopting the prose as current design guidance

R1 — Incorrect attribution of the Graetz-number omission. [medium]
RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt:57–59 says both added channel groups are absent from Bonart.
Bonart et al. explicitly define Gz = vbar Rc^2/(Dc L) in the paragraph after
Eq. (4), and justify neglected radial concentration gradients with small Gz or
low species flux. They also mention mass-transfer coefficients as an extension.
The implementation's well-mixed reduction is real; an absent discussion in the
paper is not. Replace with: “PF0 and the reduced calculation neglect lumen-side
radial resistance. Bonart discusses the Graetz-number condition for this
approximation; here we evaluate an illustrative film correction explicitly.”
The slit and cylindrical Gz conventions need not have identical numerical
prefactors. Report:82–83 is substantially consistent with the paper's chosen
reduction; it does not repair the stronger earlier absence claim.
Primary source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-53301-0
Locator: Modeling tissue and supply network, paragraph after Eq. (4).
Read via indexed publisher text on 17 October 2026; direct PMC retrieval showed
a browser challenge. No copied article or source-table redistribution is needed.

R2 — Pump/body-scale conclusion is unsupported. [medium]
RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt:181–184 reports illustrative pressures, then concludes pump
cost “only binds at body scale.” A small pressure does not establish a permissible
power budget, pumping efficiency, interconnect/valve losses, or tolerance to
obstruction. Changing h matters as approximately h^-3 in the assumed resistance;
neither a body-size boundary nor a universal negligible-cost criterion was run.
The equal-power comparison itself already demonstrates a performance constraint
at the modeled module size. Replace the final sentence with: “The chosen small
channels have low illustrative pressure drops; whether pumping is limiting
requires an explicit system power/pressure budget and omitted network losses.”
Do not discard the useful relative hydraulic comparison: with identical N
channels, uniform parallel has power R Q^2/N and series has N R Q^2, hence N^2
at equal total flow. At equal power the series flow must be Q/N. These are
correct conditional identities, not measured pump costs.

R3 — Proposed observations do not by themselves identify q, D and P. [medium]
RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt:194–200 overstates the identifiability of absolute parameters
and the sufficiency of two flows. If beta is unknown and only free O2 is observed,
the simultaneous transformation (q,D,P,beta) -> lambda*(q,D,P,beta), with Km
unchanged, leaves tissue and lumen concentration fields unchanged at EVERY flow.
Both sides of D C'' = q C/(Km+C), J = P(Cb-Cwall), and Q beta Cb' = -W J scale
equally. Thus even perfect profiles and free inlet/outlet values cannot recover
that absolute scale. Absolute uptake is Q beta (Cin-Cout) only if this content law
and beta are independently known; for a real carrier use total oxygen content.
Even with known beta, the assumed constant film law identifies P_eff through
1/P_eff = 1/P_wall + 1/kL; it does not identify both constituent resistances.
Curvature constrains q/D conditional on Km and the concentration regime, and a
single axial cross-section is not the integral uptake of a depleted whole channel.
Retain the proposed observables as useful inputs, but first state known content
law, geometry, Km/partition assumptions, profile location and measurement model;
then conduct a parameter-rank/profile-likelihood or sensitivity analysis. A
second flow can add information but is not a guarantee of separability. Ranking
volumetric uptake as the single most informative quantity also needs prior
uncertainty and sensitivity assumptions: its occurrence in a dimensionless
group alone does not establish that ranking. This is an inference-design
correction, not a proposed physical measurement protocol.

R4 — Extra beta in the written max-min equation. [low; documentation only]
stage_transport.py:243–248 writes sum_i WL/[beta T_i(tau)] = Q*beta_total.
T has units s/m and WL/T is the required Q*beta, so either write
sum_i WL/T_i(tau) = (Q*beta)_total, or, for a common known beta,
sum_i WL/[beta T_i(tau)] = Q_total. Lines 250–261 and qbeta_for_y:217–223
implement the first, correct equation. Do not “repair” numerical output to match
the erroneous docstring.

R5 — Qualify the estimated permeability optimum. [low; numerical description]
RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt:113 gives P*=0.0140/0.0139 mm/s as optima. In
pf0_crosscheck.py:241–255, these are vertices of a three-point quadratic in log P
around the largest of thirteen coarse samples. The reported value at P* is also
the fitted quadratic value, not a fresh transport solution at that P. Label these
“coarse-scan quadratic estimates of an interior optimum.” The independently
derived dilute formula is exact within its limit; the nonlinear fitted values
have no saved optimizer residual or local refinement certificate. The displayed
nonmonotone sampled values remain evidence for reversal without locating the
continuous optimum precisely.

R6 — Narrow two broad interpretation sentences. [low]
RED_LOCAL_REPORT.txt:175–176: “near” the transverse ceiling still permits a small
gain; the report itself gives a 0.5% median in that regime. Say improvements are
bounded by the remaining ceiling gap, rather than that no improvement helps.
Lines 177–180: the observed series advantage over the best split does not show
that topology “matters more” than split. The report's own rounded example at
line 159 is 0.083 -> 0.113 for uniform-to-max-min, then 0.113 -> 0.114 for
max-min-to-series. Say the tested series heuristic weakly outperformed max-min
at equal flow in the declared grid. Preserve the equal-power qualification and
the nonlinear ordering failures. Lines 87–89 also say lower q and lower D act
in the same direction while canceling; their effects on q/D are opposite. The
given approximately 0.53–1.4 ratio is consistent with opposite effects.

R7 — Add convergence and achieved-power certificates before extending the solver.
[low; reuse safeguard, not a demonstrated failure of these saved rows]
al1_scan.py:390–398 iterates equal-power max-min at most sixty times, but exhaustion
does not fail and C1–C8 at 486–497 omit its residual. The saved maximum fixed-point
residual is 9.72333324966712e-13, which is below the declared 1e-12 target, so this
is not evidence that the current grid failed to converge. Future outputs should
record convergence, actual reduced total/branch flows, achieved R*max(Qi)*sum(Qi),
and its relative error against R*Qref^2/N. The main CSV's pressure/power/flow
columns describe the equal-flow solution; its final equal-power oxygen column

Similarly, ModuleTable's ceiling/Newton and interpolation loops do not enforce

Mathematics accepted in this static review

1. SI conversion 1 uL/min = 1e-9/60 m3/s is correct (stage_transport.py:33).
   q is mol m^-3 s^-1; J is mol m^-2 s^-1; P is m/s. Q*beta*dCb/dz and W*J
   both have mol m^-1 s^-1. beta scales advected oxygen inventory only; it is
   not inserted into tissue D or the free-concentration wall driving force.

2. Shooting from the back face reverses x. Accordingly p grows positive and
   J=D*p at the wall (37–65), consistent with J=-D*dC/dx in the original wall-to-
   back coordinate. The sensitivity equation q*Km/(Km+C)^2 times v is correct.
   Defining T(s)=integral_{Cb(s)}^{Cin} dCb/J gives units s/m; adding WL/(Qbeta)
   is the correct separable axial step. Series chaining advances the same bulk
   concentration, not a fresh inlet at every module (202–215, 265–273).

3. Fixed-inlet slab minima supply a legitimate ceiling for this model. A fresh-
   inlet parallel max-min target tau requires fi(tau)=Wi Li/Ti(tau) in each
   branch. fi increases with tau; the optimum at finite available capacity-flow
   solves sum fi = Qbeta. Its use is conditional on independent positive-demand
   modules, common inlet and continuously adjustable flow. Random allocations
   are a corroborating finite test, not the reason for optimality.

4. The sorted series theorem in the two linearizable limits is valid. In zero
   order set Ui=qi Wi Hi Li and di=qi(Hi/Pi+Hi^2/(2Di)). At parallel optimum,
   sum Ui/(Cin-di-tau)=Qbeta. Sort decreasing di. For any prefix ending at j,
   denominators for i<=j are no greater than Cin-dj-tau, so
   sum_{i<=j}Ui/(Cin-dj-tau) <= sum_i Ui/(Cin-di-tau) = Qbeta.
   Therefore Cin-dj-sum_{i<=j}Ui/(Qbeta) >= tau. This is exactly the series
   minimum formula. Adjacent interchange shows decreasing di minimizes the
   maximum cumulative depletion plus transverse loss. In first order replace
   Ui with Ki Wi Li, di with ln(Ai), and Cin-tau with ln(Cin/tau), where
   Ai=(1+ai/Pi)cosh(phi_i). The same argument applies to negative log oxygen.
   Nonlinear Michaelis-Menten ordering is not proved by this argument. The
   saved 68 heuristic-order failures are an important retained limit.

5. Equal-power max-min uses the restrictive common-pressure manifold assumption:
   Delta p=R*max(Qi), power=R*max(Qi)*sum(Qi). At a fixed target, using the
   minimal feasible Qi in every branch minimizes this power; searching the
   max-min allocation family is therefore legitimate within that assumption.
   Pump efficiency, unequal resistances and valve/network losses are outside it.
   Two-sided geometry keeps tissue volume but adds exchange access and a fifth
   channel; its benefit is not a no-cost fixed-hardware comparison.

6. For first order, a=sqrt(D k1)tanh(H sqrt(k1/D)), K=(1/P+1/a)^-1, and the
   back concentration is Cin exp(-K WL/Qbeta)/[(1+a/P)cosh(phi)]. Its P-dependent
   factor is K exp(-K S), giving P*=a/(aS-1) only if aS>1. The no-finite-optimum
   branch is correctly retained. The zero-order fully penetrated expression is
   also dimensionally and algebraically consistent. These limits check model
   equations, not physiological parameter values.

Verification boundaries

The C8 mesh-change value covers the declared base-geometry/P=0.05 subset and
three arrangements (al1_scan.py:466–483), not all film/two-sided/equal-power
conditions or every ordering counterexample. Co-refined PF0 Richardson estimates
are useful estimates, not a separately measured convergence order in each axis.
No discrepancy is inferred solely from these bounded scopes.
