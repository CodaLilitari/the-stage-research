ITEM 4  Identifiability (report lines 194-200): ACCEPT
  The model is invariant under (q, D, P, beta) -> lambda (q, D, P, beta):
    - D C'' = q C/(Km+C) gains a factor lambda on both sides;
    - -D C'(0) = P (Cb - C(0)) gains a factor lambda on both sides;
    - Q beta dCb/dz = -W J gains a factor lambda on both sides.
  So every free-O2 field is unchanged at every Q.
  So the symmetry proves an absolute-scale ambiguity.
  It does not prove that the proposed sampled measurements identify every remaining group. Free-O2 profiles
  plus free inlet/outlet O2 at two or more flows can constrain the invariant groups: Phi2 (q/D), Bi (P_eff/D),
  kappa and the transfer number. Their unique practical recovery depends on:
    - sampling;
    - the concentration range covered (kappa needs C ~ Km);
    - the known content law and geometry;
    - measurement uncertainty.
  Breaking the scale ambiguity needs at least one known absolute quantity. Known total inlet and outlet O2
  content plus metered Q gives net uptake directly, but that is a separate matter from whether q, D and P are
  uniquely recoverable. pO2 readings also need the solubility in each phase.
  With a constant film closure, at most P_eff can be constrained; separating P_wall from k_L needs one more
  independent quantity. My section 6 over-claimed; corrected text is in the addendum.
