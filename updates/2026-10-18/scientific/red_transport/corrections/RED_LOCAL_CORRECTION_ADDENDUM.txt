C. DERIVATIONS BEHIND "PROVEN IN THE TWO LIMITS" (documentation; no new computation)
Setup: N modules share W, L, inlet Cin, capacity beta and total flow Q, and each module's minimum sits at its
outlet back face.

(i) Zero-order kinetics, fully penetrated (C >> Km throughout)
  Module i has uptake U_i and local drop delta_i, both independent of the lumen concentration.
  Its minimum = (its outlet bulk concentration) - delta_i.
  Parallel, with shares Q_i summing to Q: min_i = Cin - U_i/(Q_i beta) - delta_i.
    At the max-min optimum every min_i equals tau. Define x_i = Cin - delta_i - tau = U_i/(Q_i beta) > 0.
    Then sum_i U_i/x_i = Q beta.
  Series, in order of decreasing delta (that is, increasing x):
    module k minimum = Cin - S_k/(Q beta) - delta_(k), where S_k = sum_{j<=k} U_(j).
    Q beta = sum_i U_i/x_i >= sum_{j<=k} U_(j)/x_(j) >= S_k/x_(k).
    Hence S_k/(Q beta) <= x_(k), so every series minimum is >= tau.

(ii) First-order kinetics
  Module i has transfer coefficient K_i and attenuation A_i = (1 + a_i/P_i) cosh(phi_i).
  Its minimum = (its outlet bulk)/A_i, and its outlet bulk = (its inlet) x exp(-K_i W L/(Q_i beta)).
  Parallel optimum: x_i = ln(Cin/(A_i tau)) = K_i W L/(Q_i beta), so sum_i K_i W L/x_i = Q beta.
  Series, in order of decreasing A (increasing x):
    module k minimum >= tau  if and only if  (W L/(Q beta)) sum_{j<=k} K_(j) <= x_(k).
    This follows from the same chain of inequalities as in (i).

(iii) Ordering among series chains
  In both limits the series worst value is a function of max_k (sum_{j<=k} p_j + t_k):
    zero order:  worst = Cin - max_k (...),        with p = U/(Q beta) and t = delta;
    first order: worst = Cin exp(-max_k (...)),    with p = K W L/(Q beta) and t = ln A.
  Minimising max_k (completion time + tail) over orderings is solved by putting tails in non-increasing order
  (Jackson's rule; adjacent-exchange argument). So "most-limited first" is optimal in both limits, and only
  there. Michaelis-Menten kinetics break this additive separation, consistent with E5's 68 exceptions.
