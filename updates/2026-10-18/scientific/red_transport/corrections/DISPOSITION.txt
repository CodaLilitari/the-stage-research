ITEM 4  Identifiability (report lines 194-200): ACCEPT
  The model is invariant under (q, D, P, beta) -> lambda (q, D, P, beta):
    - D C'' = q C/(Km+C) gains a factor lambda on both sides;
    - -D C'(0) = P (Cb - C(0)) gains a factor lambda on both sides;
    - Q beta dCb/dz = -W J gains a factor lambda on both sides.
  So every free-O2 field is unchanged at every Q.
  Free-O2 profiles plus free inlet/outlet O2 at two flows therefore identify only the groups Phi2 (q/D),
  Bi (P_eff/D), kappa (only if the profile spans C ~ Km) and the transfer number.
  Fixing the absolute scale also needs:
    - a known content law: beta = 1 for carrier-free medium, or a measured total-versus-free O2 curve;
    - metered Q and known W, L, H;
    - the solubility in each phase, to convert pO2 readings.
  With a constant film closure only P_eff is identified. Separating P_wall from k_L needs one more independent
  quantity. My section 6 over-claimed; corrected text is in the addendum.

ITEM 5  Interpretive bounds: ACCEPT all five; I go further on (c)
  a) Pump cost. A maximum of 48 Pa per illustrative 1-mm module path does not show that pumping "only binds at
     body scale". Withdrawn. That conclusion needs a system budget: manifolds, connectors, valves, oxygenator,
     pump efficiency and duty cycle.
  b) Near the ceiling. Gains are limited, not zero: median 0.51015% (n = 1,642). "Neither ... helps" is
     withdrawn; the help is bounded by the remaining gap to the ceiling.
  c) "Topology matters more than the split." Withdrawn, because the saved data contradict it in typical
     magnitude:
       series most-limited-first over max-min: median +3.3e-5 relative, 90th percentile +2.9%, max +188%;
       max-min over equal split, flow-limited cases: median +30.7%.
     What stands: series-first was >= max-min at equal total flow in 2,520/2,520 tested configurations,
     usually by a negligible margin, and never exceeded max-min at equal power (504/504, beta 1).
  d) Two-sided supply. It keeps tissue volume but adds a fifth channel and doubles the hotspot's exchange face
     (+25% for the patch), so it changes geometry plus access, not depth alone. E6's 102/119 and its 17
     exceptions both stand. In the strongest exception (r 4, 200 um, P0.2_wall, 1 uL/min), two-sided at
     beta 1 gave 0.00309 against the base at beta 256 with 0.0463. At supply-starved flow, capacity acting as
     a flow substitute beat the geometry change about 15-fold. No universal geometry-over-capacity claim
     survives. The ceiling statement does: no flow or capacity exceeds the transverse ceiling.
  e) Wording error (report lines 87-90). Lower q lowers Phi2 and lower D raises it, so they act oppositely
     on q/D. That opposition is why they partly cancel. "Enter in the same direction" is withdrawn.
