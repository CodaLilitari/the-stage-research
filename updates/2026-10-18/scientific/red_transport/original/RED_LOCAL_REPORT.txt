2. FLOW-VERSUS-DIFFUSION DIMENSIONLESS ANALYSIS (derived independently of PF0's code)
Model class, identical to PF0. C is FREE O2 in mol/m3. Tissue slab 0<x<H: D C'' = q C/(Km+C). Wall:
J = -D C'(0) = P (Cb - C(0)). Back face: C'(H) = 0. Lumen: Q beta dCb/dz = -W J. Inlet Cb(0) = Cin.
Scaling c = C/Cin, xi = x/H, zeta = z/L leaves exactly four groups:
  kappa = Km/Cin                kinetic saturation                    PF0: 0.14
  Phi2  = q H^2/(D Cin)         transverse Damkohler (Thiele^2)       PF0: 0.125, 0.5, 2, 8 for H = 50-400 um
  Bi    = P H/D                 wall Biot number                      PF0: 0.125 (50 um, 0.005 mm/s) to 40 (400 um, 0.2 mm/s)
  Da_c  = q W H L/(Q beta Cin)  convective supply Damkohler           PF0: 5.9e-4 to 4.8
Channel geometry adds two more groups, both absent from PF0 and from Bonart et al.:
  Bi_L = P/k_L, with k_L = Sh D_f/d_h   lumen-side film versus wall resistance
  Gz   = Q h/(W L D_f) for a slit        lumen development (entrance) number
Exact structural results:
 a) Q and beta enter only through Q*beta, via Da_c. PF0's "flow x capacity degeneracy" is a property of the
    law, which I confirmed exactly in PF0's saved outputs.
 b) The slab response depends only on the local Cb, and the lumen ODE is separable. So the outlet obeys
    integral_{c_out}^{1} dc / j(c) = Da_c, where j = J/(qH). The worst region of a module is the back face at
    its outlet: c_min = m(c_out), with m(c) the slab-minimum characteristic. This is how the continuum code works.
 c) Infinite flow or capacity (Da_c -> 0): c_min -> m(1), the fixed-lumen CEILING. No flow and no capacity at
    the same free inlet can exceed it.
 d) Zero order (kappa -> 0, slab fully penetrated): c_min = 1 - Da_c - Phi2/2 - Phi2/Bi. PF0's local and
    supply necessary bounds are the qcrit versions of the last three terms.
 e) First order (dilute, c << kappa): c_out = exp(-NTU), NTU = K W L/(Q beta),
    K = 1/(1/P + 1/a), a = sqrt(D k1) tanh(phi), k1 = q/Km, phi = H sqrt(k1/D),
    c_min = c_out/[(1 + a/P) cosh(phi)].
 f) The wall-transfer optimum. In limit (e), c_min is proportional to K exp(-K W L/(Q beta)), which peaks at
    NTU = 1. That gives P* = a/(a S - 1) with S = W L/(Q beta), defined only when a S > 1.
    For NTU > 1, more wall permeability lowers the downstream worst region: upstream tissue extracts more,
    so the lumen arrives depleted. This is the analytic form of PF0's falsified monotonicity check, and the
    finding is preserved. In the nonlinear model the reversal also needs a partly starved slab, where higher
    P switches on extra consuming tissue. Thin unstarved slabs (50-100 um) show no reversal in the scan even
    where the first-order formula predicts one.
 g) Film: P_eff = 1/(1/P + 1/k_L). For a wide slit with one transferring wall (Sh = 4.86, d_h = 2h,
    D_f = 2e-9 m2/s): k_L = 0.097 mm/s at h = 50 um and 0.049 mm/s at h = 100 um. So a 0.2 mm/s wall acts like
    0.065 or 0.039 mm/s. For a 100 um tube (Sh = 3.66), k_L = 0.073 mm/s. Bonart et al. define the wall
    permeability as wall-only and treat the lumen as well mixed, so PF0 inherits Bi_L = 0. At 50-100 um
    channels the film is comparable to, or larger than, the quoted wall resistance. With D_f = 3e-9, k_L is
    1.5x larger. Fully developed Sh is the conservative value: Gz = 0.4-27 at PF0 flows for h = 100 um, and
    entrance effects raise k_L.
Labeled arithmetic, not a skin parameter set: skin_parameter_gap.txt quotes a fibroblast rate 8.4x below
PF0's and dense-collagen D values 4.4-11.8x below PF0's. Both enter Phi2 = n q_cell H^2/(D Cin) in the same
direction, so they partly cancel: Phi2 lands at 0.53-1.4 times PF0's value multiplied by (n / 2e14 per m3).
The unmeasured viable cell density n then decides the regime.

5. SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION (this model class only)
- Yes, the same total flow can raise the worst region when it is distributed differently, but only where the
  worst module is still flow-limited. There, an equalising (max-min) split gains a median of about 31% over an
  equal split. A naive demand-proportional split captures most of that.
- Once the worst module sits near its transverse ceiling (diffusion distance, wall and film), neither the
  split, more flow nor more carrier capacity helps. Geometry does.
- At equal flow, topology matters more than the split. A series chain with the most transport-limited module
  first never lost to the best parallel split. It reuses the driving-force surplus that a parallel layout
  discards at the outlets of high-drop modules. The edge is proven for zero- and first-order kinetics and was
  never violated numerically for Michaelis-Menten kinetics.
- Series has costs. It needs N^2 pump power with identical channels, and at equal power parallel won every
  time. It also passes each module's effluent and failures downstream, which is not modelled. At the 1 mm
  module scale the largest pressure drop in the scan is 48 Pa (series at 64 uL/min); parallel splits stay
  below 10 Pa. So pump cost only binds at body scale.
- More wall transfer is not monotonically good at low flow. The optimum sits near NTU = 1. Separately, the
  lumen-side film probably limits real 50-100 um channels before the wall does. PF1 should carry k_L
  explicitly.
- Carrier capacity is a flow substitute inside PF0's law. It cannot buy back diffusion distance.

6. NEXT MEASUREMENT THAT WOULD MOST REDUCE UNCERTAINTY
On the actual chosen construct, at known flow:
  (i)  a depth-resolved steady free-O2 profile from a perfused channel wall into the tissue, and
  (ii) inlet and outlet perfusate O2, at two or more flow rates.
In this model class the worst region depends only on kappa, Phi2, Bi (or Bi/(1+Bi_L)) and Da_c/NTU. The profile
curvature gives q/D, the jump at the wall gives the wall-plus-film transfer, and the inlet-outlet difference
gives absolute uptake, which separates q, D and P. Two flows separate transfer from demand through NTU.
Measuring q_cell, cell density, D and solubility separately multiplies their uncertainties. This measurement
captures the controlling combinations directly, and it replaces the arbitrary 0.05 line with observed local
oxygen. If only one quantity can be measured, the volumetric uptake rate n x q_cell matters most, because it
scales every Damkohler group.

7. LIMITS AND COMPLETION GAPS
- Liver-like PF0 constants and hypothetical demand multipliers. No skin calibration.
- Oxygen only. Steady state. No axial tissue diffusion, no lateral coupling between modules, no motion,
  compression or occlusion. No glucose, CO2, lactate, heat, shear or immune terms.
- The capacity law is hypothetical. Carrier viscosity, binding curves and release kinetics are not modelled.
- Series effluent and waste effects are not modelled. The hydraulic numbers are illustrative.
- The scipy solve_bvp cross-check ran only in the cloud sandbox during development. scipy was not installed in
  the local VM, and I chose not to install it.
- The film term enters AL1 as one condition only, and PF0's cases were not re-run with k_L.
- E5 and E6 failed as predeclared, and both failures are retained.
- Single-author AI work. Coda's independent checks should be attributed separately.
