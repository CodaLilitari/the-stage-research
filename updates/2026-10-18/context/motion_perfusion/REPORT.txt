MOTION, PERFUSION AND LOCAL OXYGEN: BOUNDED PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK
Coda (Codex Lily), collaborating with Richard Pemberton | 17 October 2026

Decision

Do not adopt 'nobody has quantified worst-region oxygen when bending compresses
perfusion channels' as an established novelty claim. Significant adjacent prior
art exists. This bounded search did not establish an exact predecessor measuring
the spatial minimum throughout an oxygen-consuming engineered tissue layer while
its embedded channels are narrowed by bending under a declared pumping budget.
That narrower unresolved match is not evidence of absence or priority.

Strongest existing evidence packages

S1 — Grigoryan et al., Science (2019), DOI 10.1126/science.aav9750.
The closest engineered deformation/oxygenation package found. Figure 3 links
cyclic airway distension to neighboring vessel compression and RBC-flow
redirection; panel E reports RBC oxygen saturation across flow conditions
(N=3 replicates). This measures blood oxygenation, not minimum free oxygen in
consuming tissue. It also does not isolate compression as the sole cause of
improved exchange. Public dataset D1 exists; its raw contents were not inspected.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7769170/

D1 — Dataset accompanying S1: https://zenodo.org/records/2614071
The live record lists a 14.0 GB v2 ZIP and two earlier archives of 14.4/14.5 GB.
None was downloaded because each exceeds the 25 MB task limit. The paper's
supplementary-material listing includes a 13.4 MB PDF and Movie 3 at 11.4 MB;
those were located but not downloaded or watched. Availability is not validation.

S2 — Mori, Morimoto and Takeuchi, Biofabrication, online 2018 / volume 2019,
DOI 10.1088/1758-5090/aaed12. Closest skin-specific prior art: perfused skin
equivalents under controlled stretching, with morphological comparisons.
The accessible primary abstract does not provide spatial oxygen minima.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30431022/

S3 — Golub et al., Frontiers in Physiology (2023),
DOI 10.3389/fphys.2023.1172834. Strongest accessible living-tissue time-series
comparator found: interstitial oxygen and derived consumption during contraction,
compression/recovery and rest. It samples local microscopic regions, not a global
spatial minimum or engineered bending geometry. Sensor oxygen consumption is
explicitly assessed. The primary HTML and tables were inspected.
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2023.1172834/full

S4 — Ferroni et al., Medical Engineering & Physics (2016),
DOI 10.1016/j.medengphy.2016.04.016. Cyclic squeeze pressure, porous geometry,
fluid-structure interaction and cardiomyocyte oxygen-consumption modeling are
already coupled in prior work. This is a computational predecessor, not an
independently measured spatial-oxygen dataset established by the accessed abstract.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27189671/

S5 — Valimaki et al., ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering (2025),
DOI 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.4c01845. Ratiometric oxygen images beneath perfused,
yeast-containing bone scaffolds provide a useful spatial-sensing comparator.
The work has no bending intervention; its sensor plate alters the transport
boundary. These images cannot be relabeled as unperturbed 3D tissue oxygen maps.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264768/

Next empirical constraint — data requirements, not an experiment authorization

The most useful missing input is a joint deformation-to-hydraulic-conductance
relationship and registered local-oxygen response for the SAME construct. A
future dataset should report synchronized deformation/curvature, channel
cross-sectional change, actual inlet/outlet pressure and flow, and oxygen fields
with sensor resolution/calibration and a stated observable (free dissolved O2,
bound saturation, or total oxygen content). An outlet saturation value cannot
stand in for the tissue's spatial minimum.

Record whether the controller holds flow, pressure or pump power. Narrowing under
fixed flow changes pressure cost; narrowing under fixed pressure changes flow.
Without that boundary condition, a deformation/oxygen comparison is ambiguous.
Report a spatial minimum only over a declared observed region and resolution,
with motion registration and sensor-induced transport/consumption effects
accounted for. A low percentile plus its uncertainty may be a more stable
observable than the noisiest pixel minimum; this is Coda's proposed analysis
criterion, not a validated result from these papers.

For the current project, retain Grigoryan's geometry/flow-redirection and oxygen-
saturation data as the strongest located engineered benchmark candidate, and
Golub's local tissue transients as a separate physiological boundary. Neither
supplies tissue-specific parameters for a living robotic skin layer. Do not fit
the two together as if they were the same experiment. A specific future archive
subset can be scoped after inspecting its manifest; no large download is
authorized or required by this report.

Search and access scope

The search began at 23:01:44 UTC, with a 15-minute maximum. Twenty-five targeted
queries followed mechanical-perfusion/oxygen, skin stretch, cyclic squeeze,
muscle compression and the directly relevant multivascular-paper leads. Only
the six records above are retained as decision evidence. This is not a systematic
review. sources.json separates measured, modeled and inferred outcomes and
records access/scale limits. SEARCH_LOG.json retains the query strings.

Several direct PMC/publisher opens returned a browser challenge or fetch error;
indexed primary text was used where labeled. Frontiers HTML and the Zenodo
metadata page opened successfully. No access barrier was bypassed. No full
dataset, PDF, video or article file was downloaded to the workspace; no solver,
new biological experiment, human contact or provider dispatch occurred. This
work is separate from Nua's release-kinetics dataset task and makes no carrier
release-kinetics claim.
