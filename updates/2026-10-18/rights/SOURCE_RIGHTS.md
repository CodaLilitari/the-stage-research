# Source rights and license provenance

This update adopts the existing project's MIT-code / CC-BY-4.0-authored-prose split only to the extent the releaser holds applicable rights. It makes no legal ownership finding from an AI contributor label. The file-specific map controls. Collaborator assent is not a grant of publisher/provider rights, scientific acceptance or provider endorsement.

**Primary measured data:** Banerjee U, Wolfe S, O'Boyle Q, Cuddington C, Palmer AF (2022), “Scalable production and complete biophysical characterization of poly(ethylene glycol) surface conjugated liposome encapsulated hemoglobin (PEG-LEH).” PLOS ONE 17:e0269939, [article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0269939), [S1 supporting data](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0269939.s001). The inspected article copyright notice links to [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the [PLOS license policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/licenses-and-copyright) and workbook/caption review support that reuse basis. No separate restrictive workbook notice was identified. The unchanged workbook SHA256 is `d08f76ad5fa957c9200edcffdeb49a7227e568f7933f63d2bd869528dae205bd`. Nua extracted 2,968 experimental rows; the CSV is a reformatted/extracted derivative, not a new measurement or CC0 dedication. Published Fit rows remain in the unchanged source workbook but are excluded from the experimental-row extraction and all fits in this project. BioRender Figure 1 and article HTML/PDF are excluded. Coda's [primary-source review](../scientific/nua_pf_kdata1/qa/PRIMARY_REVIEW.txt) records read depth and limitations.

**Executable examples in project-authored documents:** original runnable code, shell-command examples and executable interface schemas embedded in documents use MIT, to the extent applicable rights are held; surrounding prose/reports use CC BY 4.0. The affected file rows explicitly note this exception. This preserves Lilitari's original code/test/command/schema condition and applies the same code-versus-prose distinction to Coda's public reproduction guide and other original project command examples. Mathematical expressions alone, numerical fixtures and result tables are not automatically executable examples. Publisher/source-data terms remain separate.

**Dependencies:** NumPy and SciPy use BSD-style licenses; mpmath has its own BSD license; Matplotlib has its own license terms. No dependency code, wheel, runtime or installer is redistributed here. Requirements name the versions used in the local review environment. Readers obtain dependencies from their chosen legitimate distribution and preserve those distributions' notices. Importing a dependency does not relicense its source as project code.

**Historical source provenance:** hashes from original freezes and audit receipts are preserved as historical references even when a public-copy transformation changes bytes. The public manifest is the current inventory; the export map records original/export hashes and change categories. Privacy sanitization is not a new pre-run freeze or a numerical replay. No model or data-source approval is implied by an integrity hash.

Standard MIT and CC BY 4.0 legal texts were copied unchanged from the October 15 public release. Its MIT notice names Richard Pemberton and contributors to the extent of rights held. The historical October 15 source-rights map is not a blanket license for newly supplied third-party data; the separate Banerjee provenance above applies here.
