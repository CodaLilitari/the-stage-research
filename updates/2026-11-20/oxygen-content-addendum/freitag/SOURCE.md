# Sources and replay

* Standl T et al. J Vasc Surg 2003;37:859–865, Tables I and III. Training observations use mean limb flow and arterial/venous blood oxygen contents with published SEM from six animals per group. [Primary publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12663989/) · [Author/publisher article PDF copy](https://biosbc.com/storage/attachment/eN2JixLBXOwxzm5PIriuN3Bvjm6gwOMAq4ljcFEl.pdf). Training PDF SHA-256 917ce2a3397fb5359ea2792902d3a0638f0814ef8a7e991839cfcca13b49df4e. Save the original as standl.pdf. The replay extracts the ten mean and SEM triplets from the source file. Each group is in published order, and triplets are flow, arterial content, venous content.
* Freitag M et al. Can J Anesth 2005;52:904–914, Tables II, III and IV and calculation appendix. Target values use printed means and SD from six animals per group. [Primary publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF03022050) · [Article PDF](https://biosbc.com/storage/attachment/zaPDrQvWyZbBVRejqYeijdpu51AX2eWaKcrsz4Xj.pdf). Save the PDF as freitag.pdf in the replay directory. SHA-256 22d0c8d1129b0cb7c5644e110b343d91b38e0aaae95b98198c0b260c73e7108c.

Run `python replay.py` with Python 3, NumPy and Poppler’s pdftotext on PATH. The script creates the column-limited external features and eight fixed forecasts first. After verifying the forecast hash, it reads the venous values for scoring and reconstructs the compartment consistency checks. Original published charts and PDFs are not redistributed. All measurements are historical aggregate observations in dogs.
