THE STAGE HUMAN OXYGEN CARRIER TRACK
15 October 2026 | Coda collaborating with Richard Pemberton

RESULT AND RESEARCH QUESTION

Human artificial blood is a serious research track, but this first artifact is
an oxygen-carrier review and a theoretical binding calculation. It does not
establish a complete replacement for blood or a perfusion system for living
tissue over a robot. The concrete question studied here is: how do affinity
and binding-site capacity change the equilibrium oxygen-content difference
between two fixed pressures? This is useful because a carrier can load oxygen
well yet release little across a particular pressure interval.

WHAT THE HUMAN EVIDENCE SUPPORTS

The 2022 first-human HbV report concerns 11 healthy volunteers, including one
whose infusion was stopped after a rash. Fever and infusion reactions occurred;
reported events resolved. The published pharmacokinetic estimate came from a
very small evaluable subset. This supports an initial safety/kinetic observation
under the tested conditions. It is not a patient efficacy trial, evidence of
whole-blood replacement, or a demonstration of long-term tissue perfusion. The
vesicle approach is intended to compartmentalize hemoglobin; that intention
must not be restated as elimination of every toxicity. [H1]

The 2026 publication describes a planned phase Ib study in 16 healthy volunteers.
Its primary outcome is safety and its secondary outcome is pharmacokinetics.
It does not report the completed phase Ib outcomes. A small open-label,
single-centre healthy-volunteer study cannot by itself establish benefit in
hemorrhage or chronic living-tissue support. [H2]

Current registry routes describe the study as Not Recruiting and retain a
target of 16. Indexed and live routes display different update dates; each
observation is preserved in source_read_notes.txt. The root session observed
blank result fields on its live 15 October 2026 portal view. Neither that label
nor the absence of posted results is evidence of efficacy or a verdict on the
trial's outcome. [H3]

HC0 THEORETICAL MODEL

S(P) = P^n / (P^n + P50^n)
Loaded normalized content = B * S(P_load)
Residual normalized content = B * S(P_unload)
Equilibrium content difference = B * [S(P_load) - S(P_unload)]

P50 is the half-saturation pressure; lower P50 means higher affinity within
this fixed Hill model. n is an empirical-shape parameter treated here as an
assumption, not an estimated number of binding sites. B is dimensionless
relative binding-site capacity. It is not concentration, mass, volume or dose.
The content difference describes two equilibrium states; it is not a delivery
rate, and it is not proof that a tissue can extract that amount in transit.

All settings are hypothetical. The sweep covers P50 from 5 to 100 mmHg in
unit steps; n = 1, 2, 2.7 and 4; loading pressures 60, 80 and 100 mmHg;
unloading pressures 10, 20 and 40 mmHg; and capacities 0.5, 1 and 2.
These are sensitivity settings, not clinical targets or measured HbV values.
The run contains 10,368 CSV rows, representing 3,456 affinity/pressure/shape
cases evaluated at three capacity scalings. These are deterministic model
evaluations, not experimental subjects or statistical replications.

WHY THERE IS AN INTERIOR OPTIMUM

For fixed positive pressures L > U and unchanged n > 0, set A=L^n, B0=U^n,
and x=P50^n. The saturation difference becomes

  D(x) = (A-B0)*x / [(A+x)*(B0+x)].

Its derivative has the sign of A*B0-x^2. Therefore the unique interior maximum
is x=sqrt(A*B0), or P50=sqrt(L*U), independently of n. Very high affinity
leaves both states nearly saturated; very low affinity loads little. This is
an algebraic property of the imposed model, not an optimized biological design.

For the illustrative 100-to-20 mmHg interval with n=2.7, the analytic maximum
is P50=44.72136 mmHg and the saturation difference is 0.79555. Capacities
0.5, 1 and 2 give normalized content differences 0.39777, 0.79555 and 1.59110.
The last value exceeds one because it is in relative capacity units, not a
saturation fraction. Linearity in capacity is built into the equation; its
verification checks implementation consistency, not a new biological finding.

ACTUAL EXECUTION AND CHECKS

The source and configuration were frozen locally before execution. All 11,973
evaluations of declared invariant checks passed, including saturation bounds,
monotonicity, half-saturation, n=1 closed-form agreement, pressure-scale
invariance, invalid-input rejection, capacity scaling and 36 analytic optimum
cases. The same implementation reproduced CSV and JSON results exactly.
These counts describe numerical checks, not independent scientific evidence.
The freeze is local, without an independent timestamp or preregistration.

The plot was visually inspected. A separate plot_hc0.py adjusts publication
layout using the unchanged CSV; it does not change frozen hc0.py or model
results. HC0_binding_unloading.png and .pdf are the final figures.

WHAT THIS MODEL LEAVES OUT

It omits transit kinetics, flow, viscosity, vessel geometry, dissolved oxygen,
consumption feedback, pH/CO2/temperature shifts, oxidation and methemoglobin,
nitric-oxide interactions, complement/immune effects, clearance, coagulation
and cellular blood functions. The imposed downstream pressure is especially
strong: real tissue oxygen pressure depends on supply, diffusion and demand.
A favorable equilibrium content difference cannot establish compatibility,
clinical safety, oxygen delivery, tissue viability, or superiority of a carrier.

USEFUL NEXT QUESTION

The next scientific step is to obtain a source-pinned, experimentally measured
binding curve and independently characterized transport/kinetic limits for one
specified research carrier, then ask whether conclusions survive measured
uncertainty and explicit loading constraints. A later computational interface
could pass a validated content-versus-pressure function into a coupled flow and
tissue model. HC0 supplies a mathematical test case for that interface; its
settings are not measured inputs for the parallel synthetic-circulation track.
No clinical dosing, formulation, manufacture, tissue experiment or deployment
is specified or authorized by this calculation.
