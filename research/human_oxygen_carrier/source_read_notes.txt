Numerical model provenance
No numerical HC0 parameter was extracted from these trials. Clinical evidence
motivates the topic only. HC0's Hill equation, parameter grid, capacities and
fixed pressures are declared modeling assumptions. Its mathematical outputs
must not be attributed to NMU-HbV or to a real person's physiology.
