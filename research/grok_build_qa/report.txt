Material counterexamples actually executed
1. Linear resistance and fixed pressure: demand 1e-16 mol/s, content 1 mol/m3,
   extraction fraction 1, efficiency 1, zero power budget, and positive
   resistance or pressure 1. The original returns feasible interval [0,0].
   Its absolute flow tolerance of 1e-15 m3/s clamped a positive minimum flow
   down to zero. The correction returns no feasible interval. Likewise it
   no longer clamps an inverted positive interval into a feasible singleton.
2. Local oxygen delivery: at zero flow, a stagnant branch, or zero transfer
   ceiling, a positive demand of 1e-16 mol/s is marked supplied because the
   original adds 1e-15 mol/s to delivery. Each must fail; the correction does.
   These are small but nonzero demands, chosen to expose a scale-dependent
   logical error, not measured tissue rates.
3. Power: Q=1e-8 m3/s, R=1 Pa*s/m3 and eta=1 require 1e-16 W, but the original
   marks a zero power budget sufficient. At Q=1e-6 it accepts 1e-12 W against
   a 1e-15 W budget: a thousandfold excess. The combined model flag can also
   be true at zero power budget. The correction rejects these cases.
4. Outlet concentration: Q=1 m3/s, Cin=0, Cout=1e-16 mol/m3 returns negative
   delivered oxygen (-1e-16 mol/s) because of the outlet tolerance. Within
   this extraction-only model, that input now raises ValueError.
5. Malformed inputs: nonfinite or negative values can be silently admitted;
   a NaN flow fraction can even yield a true local-supply flag. Mismatched
   series arrays are silently truncated by zip. The correction validates
   the nonnegative finite pump/extraction domain and all array elements
   before evaluating flags, and rejects mismatched series arrays.

There are 10 failing tolerance/physical-boundary cases and 11 failing
input-domain/schema cases in the original, plus 8 passing controls. These are
21 counterexamples, not 21 independent biological findings. Signed pump work,
oxygen generation and arbitrary extreme floating-point magnitudes lie outside
the declared model; their rejection is an input-contract decision.

What survives the review
For steady incompressible flow with constant efficiency eta and resistance R,
P_hyd = delta_p*Q and P_elec = R*Q^2/eta. Total incoming oxygen content C gives
the one-pass capacity Q*C. With usable fraction alpha, global demand M needs
Q >= M/(alpha*C), while a power budget permits
Q <= sqrt(eta*Pmax/R). For positive M and R, the derived Gamma is
alpha^2*C^2*eta*Pmax/(M^2*R); Gamma >= 1 characterizes a nonempty global
interval in exact arithmetic. It does not guarantee any branch's supply.

At the original reference assumptions, Qmin=2e-6 m3/s and
Qmax=2.2360679775e-4 m3/s, with Gamma=12500. Only 18 of the 105 normal-grid
rows meet every declared inequality, all in the open/reference-content
scenario. Increasing carrier content does not repair a separately imposed
stagnant branch or transfer ceiling. This qualitative result survives the
correction; the ceilings themselves are assumptions, not measured transport.

The independent Decimal checks cover hydraulic/electrical power, both flow
bounds, Gamma, three serial deliveries, their sum and end-to-end conservation,
and the conditional unit conversion. The hypothetical interpretation of
40 SLPM as 40 actual liquid L/min at 100 psi gives 459.650486 W hydraulic
power. Neither that interpretation, the device claims, nor simultaneous
operation was verified. It is not a pump calibration or specification.

A further prose qualification: adding segment delivery to end-to-end delivery
double-counts the same oxygen, but need not exceed incoming carrier capacity.
For Q=C=1 and two drops of 0.1, correct total delivery is 0.2; double-counting
gives 0.4, still below capacity 1. Conservation accounting is required even
when a capacity-bound check passes.

Corrections and numerical policy
The original remains unchanged. build_corrected.py makes a separately labeled
derivative with strict supply/power/interval comparisons and consistent input
validation. Binary64 roundoff can conservatively reject a near-boundary point;
the code does not manufacture a feasible point by adding dimensioned supply.
The flow-fraction sum retains a dimensionless 1e-12 input-roundoff tolerance.
This is not an arbitrary-precision proof or a guarantee over every finite
floating-point magnitude. Overflow and subnormal-limit behavior are not
established by this bounded test suite.

Use in The Stage
Retain this as an early necessary-condition check before the coupled channel,
wall-transfer and tissue-diffusion calculation. Call the reported survival
field a model-constraint flag in prose. The meaningful next integration is to
derive resistance, branch flows, local transfer and total tissue demand from
the same explicit geometry and constitutive assumptions, then require both
global and local mass/energy balances. An independently imposed local ceiling
cannot establish a maintainable tissue-support design. Total oxygen content
must remain separate from free dissolved concentration and binding/unloading
kinetics. No tissue, blood formulation, pump hardware or clinical system was
created, measured or validated by this calculation.
