SKIN PARAMETER GAP — bounded primary-source assessment, 2026-10-15
Prepared by the local Coda team for Richard Pemberton. Research only; no PF1 run.

Decision
The next oxygen model should have separate dermal scaffold, viable epidermis,
and outer barrier layers, with an explicitly air-exposed or covered surface.
There are useful measured human-cell demand and matrix-transport anchors, but
this search did not establish a single matched dataset supplying demand,
diffusivity, solubility, oxygen dependence, and functional thresholds for the
same engineered-skin construct. Replacing all liver-like PF0 parameters with
one alleged "skin parameter set" would overstate the evidence.

1. Human dermal fibroblast oxygen demand — best immediate candidate
Streeter & Cheema, Analyst 2011, 136:4013–4019.
https://doi.org/10.1039/C1AN15249A
https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2011/an/c1an15249a
Primary publisher abstract inspected. Oxygen tension was measured at positions
inside 3D native type-I collagen scaffolds while cell density was varied;
consumption was then inferred with a mathematical model. The reported mean
human dermal fibroblast rate is 1.19e-17 mol cell^-1 s^-1. This is a measured-
profile/model-derived rate, not a direct single-cell measurement or necessarily
the Michaelis–Menten maximum. It is about 8.4 times lower than PF0's 1e-16
mol cell^-1 s^-1 liver-like assumption (our arithmetic).
Donor count, donor dispersion, passage, temperature, full density range,
uncertainty, and fitted oxygen-dependence parameters were not verified here;
the full article route returned an access barrier. No raw dataset was recovered.
Use as a provisional fibroblast-specific sensitivity anchor only. Do not pair
it automatically with PF0's liver cell density or retain its Km without a label.

2. Human epidermal demand — useful independent but indirect anchor
Balu et al., Biophysical Journal 2013, "In Vivo Multiphoton NADH Fluorescence
Reveals Depth-Dependent Keratinocyte Metabolism in Human Skin."
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3540245/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23332078/
Primary indexed article text inspected; direct full-text requests challenged.
During arterial occlusion, optical hemodynamic measurements and NADH changes
were combined to estimate near-basal keratinocyte demand: approximately
0.035 micromol per 10^6 cells per hour, equivalent to 9.72e-18 mol cell^-1 s^-1
(our unit conversion). The estimate uses an approximately 200-picoliter imaged
region containing about 200 cells. It is not a whole-epidermis or cultured-skin
qmax measurement, and depends on the optical/inference assumptions. Donor
distribution and uncertainty around this particular estimate were not verified.
Keep it separate from the fibroblast estimate; similar numbers do not establish
equal demand across cell types, differentiation states, culture ages, or injury.

3. Oxygen diffusivity of an engineered dermal matrix
Cheema et al., J Tissue Engineering and Regenerative Medicine 2012, 6:77–84.
https://doi.org/10.1002/term.402
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21312340/
Primary abstract inspected. Optical-fiber core oxygen monitoring and Fick-law
fitting gave D = 4.5e-6 cm^2/s for 11% collagen density, 1.7e-6 cm^2/s for 34%,
and 3.4e-6 cm^2/s for photochemically crosslinked 11% collagen. In SI these are
4.5e-10, 1.7e-10, and 3.4e-10 m^2/s. These are material-specific dense collagen
measurements, not native-human-dermis constants or values for a dilute gel.
They are 4.4–11.8 times smaller than PF0's 2e-9 m^2/s (our arithmetic).
Full methods, precise density definition, temperature, replicates, and raw
transients were not verified in this bounded pass. Preserve that uncertainty;
do not map a stated collagen mg/mL concentration onto these percentages without
checking the definitions and final hydrated construct state.

4. The outer skin barrier has a distinct diffusivity
Hatcher & Plachy, Biochimica et Biophysica Acta 1993, 1149:73–78.
https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-2736(93)90026-V
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8391321/
Primary abstract inspected. EPR time-dependent oxygen line broadening gave
human stratum-corneum D = 3e-7 cm^2/s at 37 C = 3e-11 m^2/s; approximate
activation energy 44 kJ/mol. This isolated outer-barrier measurement must not
be assigned to living dermis. Donor numbers, hydration conditions and variance
were not recovered. Oxygen solubility/partitioning is separately needed to
turn D into pressure-based conductance; diffusivity alone is not permeability.

5. Public human measurements constrain the external boundary, not total demand
Stucker et al., Journal of Physiology 2002, 538:985–994.
https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2001.013067
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2290093/
Primary full-text methods and Table 1 inspected. The public table gives
individual measurements for 20 healthy volunteers: ten aged under 30 and ten
over 70. At the humidified volar forearm, 33 C, surface pO2 163 +/- 9 Torr,
external uptake was 0.53 +/- 0.27 mL O2 min^-1 m^-2 (mean +/- SD). Surface
preparation included removal of superficial horny scales; this is not an
untouched, dry, covered, or submerged skin boundary. Five-minute blood-flow
interruption increased this external flux by 9.5 +/- 6.3%. The paper's
0.25–0.40 mm externally supplied depth is model-derived using other transport
data; it is not a universal viability thickness. This table is the clearest
immediately accessible donor-level numerical dataset found here. Use it to
check an air-exposed model's surface flux, not as whole-skin oxygen demand or
a specified pump flow. Retain gas-volume units until reference gas conditions
are checked for a molar conversion.

6. Functional oxygen conditions do not reduce to a universal minimum
Mieremet et al., Scientific Reports 2019, 9:7811.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44204-4
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6534609/
Primary methods/results inspected through publisher/indexed full text. Human
fibroblast/keratinocyte skin-equivalent batches used five primary-cell donors
from healthy surplus breast skin; models were compared at 3% versus 20%
external oxygen during 14–15 days at an air–liquid interface. Lower oxygen
changed proliferation, differentiation and barrier lipids, with several
features closer to native skin. This is evidence against "more oxygen always
better," not a direct tissue concentration, demand curve, safe lower bound,
or robot-skin endurance test. Raw/processed data are available on request,
not verified here as a public download. A functional endpoint and local
dissolved concentration must replace PF0's arbitrary 0.05 mol/m^3 pass line
before any claim of a skin survival or performance requirement.

7. A perfused engineered-human-skin example gives a geometry-specific flow
Rhee et al., Bioengineering 2024, 11:1055.
https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering11111055
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11591533/
Primary indexed full text inspected. A collagen/fibroblast dermis and neonatal
human keratinocyte epidermis surround printed channels, with 500-micrometer
inner edge and 400-micrometer inter-channel spacing; inlet flow is 10 microliter/
minute. The setup maintains an air–liquid interface and permits gas exchange.
This is an operating point, not a measured minimum oxygen-sufficient flow.
Fluorescein transport and tissue/response endpoints do not identify local O2
profiles, extraction, qmax, or wall O2 permeability. Experiments are generally
triplicate, not demonstrated as three independent donors; donor spread is not
established. Raw datasets are on request. The text's cell-count/concentration
statement in section 2.6 is arithmetically inconsistent, so its stated seeding
density should not become a model input without clarification. No formulation
or fabrication procedure is reproduced here.

Concrete next science input (proposal, not a new run)
Choose one explicitly defined engineered-skin geometry and culture state, then
assemble a parameter ledger with separate observed, fitted and assumed fields:
- Cell-type fractions and actual viable cell density per layer, not initial
  seeding density; donor, passage, time, temperature and differentiation state.
- q_cell(C) for fibroblasts and keratinocytes. The two demand anchors above can
  seed a sensitivity range but do not jointly identify qmax and Km.
- D and oxygen solubility/partition coefficient for each hydrated matrix and
  barrier, plus the channel-wall transfer resistance. Do not collapse them.
- Surface boundary: air, covered, or submerged; gas transfer and cover material
  belong in the boundary condition. Preserve atmospheric entry separately from
  circuit delivery in the oxygen budget.
- Measured inlet/outlet free oxygen, local tissue oxygen profiles and functional
  outcomes for the selected construct. No dataset recovered here identifies
  the minimum circulation rate that preserves living, repairing robot skin.

For computation, q_volume = sum_i(n_i * q_cell_i) has units mol m^-3 s^-1.
A necessary oxygen balance is Q*(Ctotal,in-Ctotal,out) + surface O2 input >=
integrated tissue consumption. It is necessary, not sufficient: an adequate
global balance can coexist with local hypoxia. Pressure/flow, shear, nutrient
delivery, waste removal, heat, hydration and mechanical damage need separate
quantities; oxygen-only inference does not establish full perfusion needs.

Immediate bounded follow-up should recover full methods/data for sources 1–3,
extract Table 1 of source 5 with its conditions, and select a matched skin
construct before freezing a layered model. If matched data remain unavailable,
label the next result a sensitivity/identifiability study, not a calibrated
skin prediction. No new model was run, no source authors were contacted, and
no experimental outputs were modified during this task.
