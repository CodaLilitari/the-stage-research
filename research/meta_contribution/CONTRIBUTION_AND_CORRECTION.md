# Meta AI — limited contribution and correction record

**Status: a methodological contribution with source-access and artifact-access limitations.** Meta AI helped separate repair endpoints: gap closure, barrier performance, mechanics and sensing. Its original response used incorrect leakage units and proposed unsupported quantitative acceptance criteria. Coda requested a correction; Meta acknowledged and withdrew those claims. The original erroneous response is retained privately as superseded history, not presented here as accepted science.

The correction history specifically includes withdrawal of the **41.5-fold barrier factor** and **less-than-1-mg/h acceptance criterion**. Meta said direct access to the primary correction, DOI [10.1016/j.matt.2025.102162](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matt.2025.102162), was blocked and accepted the correction supplied in Coda's packet. That packet reported one-hour masses of 0.54 g for dermis and 0.13 g for skin. Here those values identify the supplied correction, not independent source inspection by Meta or a newly validated engineering threshold. Neither the withdrawn absolute criterion nor a replacement percentage is adopted as a module acceptance rule.

Meta also corrected the distinction between a reported sample count and inferred biological replication; mean-threshold comparisons and reliability evidence; conductivity recovery and actual sensing performance; and a model-derived gap-closure bound and measured cell-tracking speed. Unverified e-skin percentages were removed from its quantitative acceptance tests. These distinctions are useful methodological contributions. Meta's remaining “verified” numerical labels and reported execution are collaborator testimony, not this project's independent verification of all source values or the native artifacts.

The native downloadable CSV, Python and JSON artifacts could not be recovered through the supported browser route. Their complete contents were therefore not available for this release's independent file-level inspection or execution. Visible discussion and partial code are insufficient to certify a complete module. No reconstructed file, partial extract or Coda implementation is attributed here as Meta's recovered native artifact. There are no Meta-derived numerical results included as independently reproduced science.
