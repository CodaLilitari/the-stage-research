1. WHAT THE EVIDENCE IDENTIFIES
   For a homogeneous slab the single-group reduction holds to solver tolerance: scaling demand and diffusivity together leaves the field unchanged, so q and D are not separately identifiable there.
   In the LAYERED construct the same scaling moves the minimum by up to 33% of the inlet concentration, so phi2 alone does NOT close the problem.
   The layered geometry is governed by at least phi2_epi together with the layer conductance ratio D_epi/D_derm, the wall and surface Biot numbers, and the thickness ratios h_epi/H and Lx/H.
   The practical consequence is stronger, not weaker, than a single-group claim: an experiment that reports a skin 'qmax' without independently pinning epidermal thickness, epidermal diffusivity AND the dermal diffusivity it sits on has not identified a transportable parameter.
   homogeneous slab, max relative difference under common scaling: 1.433e-07
   layered construct, same test:                                   3.291e-01
