SOURCE AND MODEL CORRECTIONS THAT MATTER
1. Use the primary micromole units. The printed per-cell estimate is9.7222e-18mol/cell/s; the bulk estimate is1e-4mol/m3/s; stated-volume reconstruction is1e-19mol/cell/s. Printed/reconstructed remains97.222. These are an unresolved inference discrepancy, not license to silently select or calibrate either value. The provider's assertion that the primary paper prints millimoles, and that the previous Stage read was wrong about micromoles, must be reversed.

2. Rhee's cell-count arithmetic discrepancy is real, but4e6/100uL is the suspension before mixing, not an alternative final concentration on equal footing with the200uL calculation. The stated3mg/mL is collagen stock;90uL in200uL gives nominal1.35mg/mL before contraction. Cultured-gel concentration and diffusivity remain unmeasured here.10-in/12-out pump settings do not imply outward leakage. Constant-volume liquid conservation requires an extra2uL/min inward contribution or another explanation. Actual delivered volumes and route are not established by settings alone.

3. The model is an idealized layered rectangular domain borrowing400um spacing. It applies C_lumen=.2 uniformly along an entire vertical wall, including epidermal rows. It never uses the500um lumen dimension or10/12uL/min flow in its PDE, includes no finite axial oxygen budget and does not reconstruct the printed-channel shape. Calling it Rhee's actual operating point or a fully dimensioned construct overstates what was represented. The apparently small sensitivity to total height partly reflects supplying the whole height from that wall. It does not prove lattice tightening buys nothing in actual skin.

4. The6e-17 BioNumbers value is linked to a3T3/hepatocyte study; transfer as measured HDF is questionable. However von Heimburg's primary abstract includes fibroblast comparators. The blanket claim both numbers refer to the wrong cell type is unverified without full-text mapping. This audit preserves the narrower provenance concern rather than attributing a demonstrated error to the whole database or asserting a known biological overestimate.

5. The reported 'dermal Thiele modulus' is qL²/(DC), a squared demand-diffusion group. Its full saved range is.00238–.56, not.0024–.048; the shorter range applies to the dilute-D branch only. Epidermal full range is.15–486, not.15–5 when the erroneous printed branch is included. These groups alone do not define a universal distance from diffusion limitation under Robin conditions and layered geometry. Their scaling counterexample legitimately shows one group is insufficient in this model. It is not proof of general experimental non-identifiability or that every rate measurement requires a particular set of independently measured parameters.

6. For f(C)=qmax*C/(Km+C), df/dC=qmax*Km/(Km+C)^2 decreases with C. At C=.05 and Km=.028 the derivative is12.886% of its value at zero and uptake64.103% of qmax. The linear-concentration slope is greatest at zero, and d f/d log C peaks at C=Km, not1.8Km. Classification sensitivity depends on the full transport problem and proximity to its line. The numerical Km flip survives as a scenario but does not support the stated 'steepest' explanation.

7. Stücker conductance is an assumption-dependent effective bound for prepared, humidified skin, not an intact-skin surface measurement. The16.6um D/P quotient does not establish stratum-corneum thickness independently. The9.5% occlusion-related surface-flux change does not imply atmospheric oxygen is a minority supply route. Kawai's unequal correction factors are explicitly resolved by the available author-institution erratum text; use0.54g and0.13g after1hour with sample conditions, not a universal steady rate.

8. The returned30% sealing-shift and20% budget cutoffs are analyst choices, not validated module-failure criteria. The model changes total uptake when concentration changes, so loss of atmospheric share need not transfer one-for-one to circuit uptake. A lumen mass balance with unequal measured inlet/outlet flows must start from Qin*Cin-Qout*Cout, with any other inflow/outflow, storage and oxygen exchange terms; Q*(Cin-Cout) alone assumes equal flows. A budget mismatch can also arise from measurement uncertainty, unaccounted storage/exchange or inconsistent construct comparisons. No proposed biological procedure was adopted or executed. Model checking is not a proof of tissue viability or hypoxia.
