HB-STRUCT0: HUMAN HEMOGLOBIN DEPOSITED-STRUCTURE CALIBRATION
Executed 2026-10-15; retrospective engineering/scientific calibration, not preregistered inference.

Completed: downloaded experimental oxyhemoglobin 2DN1 and deoxyhemoglobin 2DN2 from RCSB, installed Biopython 1.88 / NumPy 2.5.4 in a separate local target directory, and executed offline coordinate analysis. This is not a protein-fold prediction. No sequence or private material was uploaded, no model weights were downloaded, and no shared environment package or lock was changed. The work occupies approximately 56 MiB, including 54.5 MiB of isolated Python packages.

INPUTS AND SCOPE
RCSB entry pages and retained mmCIF metadata identify both as unmutated human hemoglobin, X-ray structures at 1.25 A from the same Park et al. 2006 study (J Mol Biol, DOI 10.1016/j.jmb.2006.05.036). The article abstract and entry metadata were inspected; the full paper and electron-density maps were not reviewed.

2DN1 asymmetric unit: alpha A, 140 modeled residues of 141; beta B, 145 of 146. Author residue 1 is absent in each chain. Two HEM, two OXY (dioxygen), two MBN (toluene), 230 water residues. 2DN2 asymmetric unit: alpha A/C, 141 each; beta B/D, 146 each; four HEM, no OXY, 221 water residues. No alternate/disordered atom objects were encountered by the parser. Deposited alpha and beta sequences match exactly across entries (141 and 146 residues).

The oxy coordinate file contains a dimer asymmetric unit and deoxy a tetramer asymmetric unit; the biological assembly is tetrameric for both. No symmetry-generated biological assembly was constructed here. Chain and specified dimer fits must not be presented as a full tetramer transition or an isolated causal effect of oxygen binding. Crystal environment and refinement contribute to coordinates.

METHOD AND EXECUTED RESULTS
One model, author chain/residue identifiers, identical amino-acid names at each matched position, common available C-alpha atoms only, equal weights, proper-rotation least-squares fit. Distances and RMSDs are in angstroms. No residues were removed after examining residuals.

These are descriptive coordinate differences, not six independent biological replicates. Maximum individual C-alpha residuals reach 6.06 A in the beta chain comparisons, so a single RMSD conceals local variation; ca_residuals.csv preserves every residual.

The six hemes were measured against their four porphyrin nitrogen atoms NA/NB/NC/ND, the nearest histidine NE2, and explicitly labeled OXY atoms. The absolute Fe distance from the least-squares plane through those four nitrogen atoms is a defined local geometric measurement, not the displacement from a whole-porphyrin plane.

Nearest histidines are alpha His87 and beta His92. All nine deposited identity-symmetry metal-contact distances were independently recomputed from coordinates within 0.002 A of the mmCIF values. A geometric radius query finds both atoms of each nearby OXY molecule within 3 A; that query is not a claim of two independent Fe-O chemical bonds. The deposited struct_conn table is retained separately inside results.json; it records five oxy and four deoxy metal contacts. No hydrogen bonding, bond order, valence, affinity, toxicity, oxygen capacity or delivery flux is inferred from these distances.

CHECKS
Six check groups passed: all four acquisition hashes; nine deposited metal distances; six fits agreeing between Biopython, explicit transformed residuals and a separate NumPy Kabsch implementation; self/rigid-transform invariance with a non-rigid perturbation positive control; expected OXY and heme inventories; exact deposited sequence identity. These are internal executable consistency checks, not external scientific replication. The fit implementations share input parsing and NumPy numerical machinery. Source entry validation and electron density have not been independently re-refined. Numbers retain machine precision in JSON/CSV for reproducibility, not as estimates of experimental precision.
