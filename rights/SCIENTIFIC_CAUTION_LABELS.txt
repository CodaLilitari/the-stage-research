THE STAGE — REQUIRED SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT FOR THIS PUBLIC SNAPSHOT
15 October 2026. Ready-to-use public wording; use the relevant paragraphs beside
the corresponding artifact and preserve source/correction provenance.

GENERAL
The first milestone is research toward artificial blood and oxygen support for
synthetic tissue systems. This snapshot contains computational benchmarks,
source audits and analyses of published data. It does not demonstrate an
artificial-blood product, a viable tissue module, a physical AI body, clinical
benefit, treatment or dosing. Numerical agreement and software checks establish
bounded computational properties; they do not establish biological validity.
Separate local Coda-team reviewers are not external experimental validators.
Original collaborator submissions, local executions, corrections and acceptance
decisions remain distinct.

MECH1 — GROK BOT PROPOSAL AND LOCAL COUPLED SCREEN
The local corrected screen assumes equal-volume annular tissue territories,
uniform zero-order uptake, selected diffusion and wall-transport constants, a
clamped inlet concentration and simplified radial/axial coupling. It examines
hydraulic constraints under assumed inputs. The 4,512-case grid and its feasible
subset are a parameter screen, not a probability or a biological success rate.
At the selected central 200-micrometre geometry, minimum channel counts 41, 53
and 44 arise under three stated wall models and imposed budgets. They are not
universal channel counts or design recommendations.

The original ten-channel calculation does not establish local oxygen supply:
its radial concentration drop exceeds the assumed inlet concentration before
axial depletion is counted. A formally negative concentration signals failure
of that zero-order scenario, not a physically realizable negative concentration.
The modeled power is pressure drop times flow, hydraulic power in the fluid;
it does not include motor, electrical or controller losses. These calculations
do not establish a universal 0.6-mm muscle thickness cap or prove that tissue
and actuators can never share a fluid. A separate-loop architecture remains a
design hypothesis. A proposed 50-fold increase in total oxygen content does not
imply 50-fold usable unloading, delivery flux or flow reduction. Carrier
kinetics, usable concentration difference and viscosity require separate data.
Performance values from different HASEL configurations cannot be combined into
one demonstrated actuator operating point.

PF1-S — COWORK CLOUD ORIGINAL AND CORRECTED SENSITIVITY
The returned original solver genuinely reproduced 972 cases with 460 above its
assumed threshold. Its source-backed demand interpretation is superseded: the
primary Balu text prints micromoles, not millimoles. Correct units give about
9.7222e-18 mol/cell/s for the printed per-cell estimate, 1e-4 mol/m3/s for the
bulk estimate and 1e-19 mol/cell/s from the stated imaging volume/cell-count
reconstruction. The remaining factor 97.222 discrepancy is unresolved. None of
these is established here as a maximal epidermal consumption rate.

The corrected 729-case grid is hypothetical sensitivity analysis, not calibrated
skin physiology. It gives 703 threshold passes on the original mesh and 702 on
a mesh aligned to the nominal 100-micrometre layer. The threshold 0.05 mol/m3 is
an analyst choice, not a survival or function threshold. One case changes from
0.05025926 to 0.04600643 mol/m3 and remains below threshold after further
refinement. Six nearest-threshold refinements provide bounded numerical checks,
not a global proof of mesh convergence. Corrected marginal pass-rate variation
is tied for demand and wall conductance over these chosen axes; the original
headline that demand alone dominates is not retained.

The solver is an idealized rectangle with a fixed-concentration lumen wall over
its full height, including epidermal rows. It has no finite axial oxygen budget
and does not reconstruct the source paper's channel or actual operating flow.
The Rhee 10-in/12-out settings require another inward contribution at constant
volume or another explanation; they do not imply outward leakage. The source's
nominal collagen dilution and unresolved cell-count arithmetic must remain
separate from a measured post-culture diffusivity. BioNumbers' HDF transfer is
questionable for one cited cell study; the blanket assertion that both numbers
are proven wrong-cell-type values is not established. Michaelis–Menten uptake
has its largest dR/dC at the lowest nonnegative concentration, not at 1.8 Km.
The proposed 30% sealing-shift and 20% budget-error cutoffs are unvalidated
analyst choices; no biological protocol or module-failure rule was adopted.
The original ledger's verified_primary labels record contributor assertions,
not the current project's blanket endorsement.

PF-DATA1 — NUA / STÜCKER REANALYSIS
These are exploratory summaries of 20 rounded published observations from
prepared, humidified skin, with the source's tape-stripping and temperature
conditions retained. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence, a fixed
absolute blood-supply mechanism, or individual linearity from two points.
Reported statistical intervals are conditional on the assumed sampling model;
the Monte Carlo mixture with analyst-selected demand is a scenario distribution,
not a confidence interval. J/q depth estimates do not establish safe tissue
thickness or channel spacing. Aggregate analysis is supplied while the complete
source transcription and individual derived output are omitted from this release.

GEMINI PARAMETER COMPILATION
The original draft is preserved for critique, not accepted as a primary dataset
or product ranking. It converts some source ranges into invented mean/SD pairs,
contains unverified reference values, and mixes 20-degree kinetic observations
with 37-degree equilibrium conditions. Its Monte Carlo spread is not calibrated
biological uncertainty. Its held-out Hill samples are synthetic, and heuristic
NO, redox and delivery penalties are not validated physiology. Source licensing
does not cure these scientific limitations.

BH0 — DRYAD REANALYSIS
The data describe actuator force, not hand grip or airborne load capacity. The
15 rows per 3A condition do not establish 15 independent subjects or pairing.
The 3B samples are three actuators repeatedly measured at eight tissue counts,
not 24 independent biological replicates or three demonstrated human donors.
These selected CSVs contain no oxygen measurements that identify the proposed
oxygen-access mechanism.

HB-STRUCT0 / HB-FOLD1 / HB-FOLD2
The deposited oxy/deoxy structures calibrate geometry and parsing. Local heme
distances and aligned C-alpha RMSD do not predict affinity, unloading, toxicity
or therapeutic function. The folds use a known public 141-residue mature alpha
chain, mapped to full-translation UniProt positions 2–142; comparisons use 140
common C-alpha atoms. This is a tool/calibration comparison, not a novel fold or
an unseen-target generalization test. The poor single-sequence result is retained.
FOLD2 submitted a 4,522-row alignment with 1:1 MSA caps; cluster profiles can
retain information from extra rows, so this is not proof that only one homolog
or only one sequence supplied the improvement. AlphaFold outputs are theoretical
predictions, not clinical validation.

OK0 — OXYGEN KINETICS
This model alternates clamped loading and unloading phases for a parcel; it is
not two finite well-mixed CSTRs. Affinity can increase through association as
well as reduced unbinding. Parameters are assumptions, not a fitted carrier.
The stable tiny-contact correction was checked in the declared range through
1e-20; it is not claimed to avoid floating-point underflow at every possible
scale. Independent local numerical QA does not imply external biological
replication or therapeutic suitability.
